## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- One explicit, renormalized $Q(s)$ at $\mathcal O(\delta^2)$ with derivatives, usable by alp-gii.4.
- An independent check of the $\Gamma_i$ of alp-gii.1 from the loop divergences.

**Non-Goals:**
- Resonance loops, resonance self-energies and tree-level resonance exchange (alp-gii.3).
- Loop corrections to ALP decay vertices (alp-gii.8), including the $a\to PPP$ and photon channels.
- Solving the pole equation, fitting inputs or numerical values of $L_i^r$ (alp-gii.4, alp-gii.5).
- Two-loop and $\mathcal O(1/f_a^2)$ graphs, including the ALP self-energy $\Sigma_{aa}$ and its $\Phi\Phi$ cut (confirmed by the user: everything stays at first order in $1/f_a$).

## Decisions

1. **Background-field computation with the general $\Sigma$ formula.** Expand $\mathcal L^{(0)}$ (kinetic term, $\chi_+$ term, $\tau X_{TC}^2$ term, with ALP sources) to quartic order in the fluctuation and background, and evaluate $\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\log H$ at second order. *Alternative:* enumerate Feynman graphs by hand. This is error-prone for the non-commuting flavour structure of the nonet.

2. **Quartic vertices from the covariant expansion.** Use the CCWZ expansion of Alda:2026nrd Appendix A, with $u=\exp(i\Phi/(2f_\pi))=\exp(i\phi)$, $\Phi=\sum\lambda_PP$, $\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_i\lambda_j)=2\delta_{ij}$ and $u^2=U$, with the ALP entering through $u_\mu^{(1)}\supset 2\partial_\mu a\,c_q^A/f_a$ and the spurions. A $\Phi^4$ vertex gives a tadpole in $\Sigma_{ij}$, and an $a\Phi^3$ vertex gives one in $\Sigma_{i\hat a}$. The vertices are generated by a sympy script, not typed in.

3. **Topology decision by enumeration.** The project has no CP-violating sources and no constant $\theta$ term, so parity forbids a $\Phi^3$ vertex. Cubic $a\Phi\Phi$ vertices do exist for flavour-violating $c_q^V$ and $c_q^S$ (user input, task 1.4). A bubble in $\Sigma_{ij}$ or in $\Sigma_{i\hat a}$ at first order in $1/f_a$ needs a $\Phi^3$ vertex on the meson leg, so both functions are expected to be tadpole-only, with polynomial $s$ dependence and no unitarity cut. The enumeration script checks this by computing the cubic and quartic terms for every source of Alda:2026nrd, and the records state the result per source. The $a\Phi\Phi$ vertices enter elsewhere: a closed $\Phi\Phi$ loop gives a term linear in $a$, and a bubble with two such vertices is a contribution to the ALP self-energy at second order in $1/f_a$. *Alternative:* include bubbles by default. This would add non-existent cuts.

4. **LO mass eigenbasis, general flavour structure.** Internal propagators are diagonal in the LO mass eigenstates of the nonet, including the $\pi^0$–$\eta$–$\eta'$ mixing of the neutral matrices. Flavour structure is carried as matrix traces in the $U(3)$ generator basis and contracted numerically only at the final tabulation. The degenerate-mass $SU(3)$ limit is the analytic check against Gasser:1984gg.

5. **Loop functions.** For tadpoles the only function is $A(m^2)=\frac{m^2}{16\pi^2}\log(m^2/\mu_\chi^2)$ after $\overline{\rm MS}-1$ subtraction, plus the pole. If bubbles are present in the extension, the finite part is the standard $\bar J(s)$ with threshold $(m_1+m_2)^2$. Both are written in terms of the pole $\lambda(\mu_\chi)$ of Gasser:1983yg, as in the scheme record. No new names for these functions are introduced without approval.

6. **Counterterm cancellation as a symbolic identity.** Collect the pole part of each entry of $\Sigma_{IJ}$, add the local terms of the quadratic Lagrangian with $L_i=L_i^r(\mu)+\Gamma_i\lambda(\mu)$ and $B=B^r(1+4\tau\lambda/F^4)$, and assert that the sum is finite and $\mu_\chi$ independent. The $\eta'$ tadpole and the $\tau$ term bring the $B$ renormalization of alp-gii.1. A mismatch with the $\Gamma_i$ table is a finding to report, not to absorb.

7. **Derivatives and residues.** $\partial_s\Sigma$ is taken symbolically. The perturbative pole shift and residue follow the first-order formula of the LSZ loop record, evaluated at the LO pole. If $\Sigma$ is tadpole-only, $\partial_s\Sigma$ is a constant matrix and the correction is a finite wave-function renormalization of the nonet.

8. **Resummation criterion.** Declare the perturbative result adequate where $|\Sigma(s)|\le\delta_{\rm est}(s)\,|Q^{(0)}(s)|$ elementwise, with $\delta_{\rm est}$ of the alp-gii.1 truncation record. Otherwise, solve the pole equation of the full $Q(s)$ (Dyson resummation of the same one-loop $\Sigma$) and label it as outside the strict order. The criterion is in the script. *Alternative:* always resum. This mixes orders without a stated reason.

9. **Record layout.** New folder `memory/nlo/loops/` with `INDEX.md` and atomic records: vertex enumeration and topologies; loop functions and subtraction; meson self-energy; mixed ALP–meson function; counterterm cancellation; thresholds and analytic structure; derivatives and pole residues; perturbative versus resummed prescription. `memory/nlo/INDEX.md` gets one topic-level entry.

10. **Delegation.** `coder` writes all scripts (`derive_nlo_loop_vertices.py`, `derive_nlo_loop_self_energies.py`) under `scripts/`. `bibliography-manager` handles any new reference. No `latex/` edits.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The $\Gamma_i$ in Kaiser:1998ds may disagree with the loop pole coefficients, especially $\Gamma_6$ ($1/16$ vs $11/144$ in $SU(3)$) and $\Gamma_{25}$] → Report the discrepancy with the derivation; do not adjust the loop to match.
- [The singlet $\eta'$ with $m_0^2=\mathcal O(\delta)$ makes the nonet tadpoles scheme-sensitive] → Keep $\tau$ and $B$ renormalization explicit, and treat $\eta'$ as light as declared in alp-gii.1.
- [Tadpole-only structure could hide a missing cubic vertex] → The enumeration runs over every source of Alda:2026nrd, including flavour-violating $c_q^V$ and $c_q^S$, and the result is stated per source.
- [Near $1\,$GeV, $\delta_{\rm est}\sim0.7$, so the perturbative correction is not reliable] → The criterion of Decision 8 and the report of both results.
- [Excluded resonance loops are $\mathcal O(\delta)$, larger than the included $\delta^2$] → Stated condition from alp-gii.1: inputs fixed from data; carried into the truncation uncertainty.

## Open Questions

- Whether the ALP–meson mixed block should also include the tadpole with the $c_G$ (gluonic) vertex at this order depends on the source specialization chosen in alp-gii.8. The records list the vertex and mark it, without changing the approach.

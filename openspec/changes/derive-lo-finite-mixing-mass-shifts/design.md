# Design

## Context

LO blocks (neutral basis $(P_3,P_8,P_0,P_6,P_7,a)$, [finite-mixing record](../../../memory/alp/shared/finite-alp-meson-mixing.md)): $K_{ij}=\delta_{ij}$, $K_{ia}=-v_i$, $K_{aa}=1+v^2$, $v_i=\epsilon\operatorname{tr}(c_q^A\lambda_i)$; $M^2_{PP}$ independent of $f_a$; $M^2_{ia}$ and $M^2_{aa}$ as given there. Existing results used as inputs: [charged decoupling](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-charged-meson-decoupling.md), [neutral block reduction and exact $K^0$ pole](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-neutral-block-reduction.md), [weak-mixing expansion](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-weak-mixing-expansion.md) (pole formulas through $\delta D^{\rm eff}_{aa}$), [single-pole alignment](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-single-pole-alignment.md), [general redefinition invariants](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-general-chiral-redefinition-invariants.md), [near-degeneracy](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-near-degeneracy-two-level.md). Sources linear in $a/f_a$ (the quadratic blocks depend on higher Taylor coefficients only through $\Delta M^2_{aa}=-\tfrac{f_\pi^2}{2f_a^2}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}\chi_2$, see the [joint-vertex record](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-joint-vertex-expansion.md)); this term is carried as one additive symbol in the ALP shift. With $M=S+iP$, $S$, $P$ Hermitian and $\chi=2B_0M$, it equals $\Delta M^2_{aa}=-\dfrac{f_\pi^2}{f_a^2}B_0\operatorname{tr}S_2$, with $S_2=d^2S/dx^2|_0$ the undressed second derivative (not the dressed $\widehat S_2$); $\operatorname{tr}P_2$ drops out.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** closed analytical shifts of $\pi^0,\eta,\eta',K^0$, $\pi^\pm$, $K^\pm$ and the ALP; exact sum rules; expression through invariants; checks against the solver and the weak-mixing record.
**Non-Goals:** decay constants, residues, mixing angles, other multiplets, NLO/loops, uncertainties, numerics (see proposal).

## Decisions

1. **Exact relations first.** $\sum m_n^2=\operatorname{tr}(K^{-1}M^2)$ holds because the poles are the eigenvalues of $K^{-1}M^2$. With $K^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1+vv^{\mathsf T}&v\\v^{\mathsf T}&1\end{pmatrix}$ the trace is $\operatorname{tr}M^2_{PP}+v^{\mathsf T}M^2_{PP}v+2v\cdot M^2_{Pa}+M^2_{aa}$. $\prod m_n^2=\det M^2$ since $\det K=1$. These give the total shift without diagonalizing, and the sign and size of the sum of the individual shifts.
2. **Expansion through the Schur complement, not matrix perturbation theory.** Meson pole: $(s-m_{n,0}^2)D^{\rm eff}_{aa}(s)=D_{na}(s)^2$ near $m_{n,0}^2$; iterate once for $O(\epsilon^4)$. ALP pole: iterate $\delta D^{\rm eff}_{aa}$. This reuses verified formulas and keeps the dependence on $s$ of $D_{ja}(s)=sK_{ja}-M^2_{ja}$ explicit (the kinetic mixing enters through $s=m_{j,0}^2$).
3. **Express through invariants.** $D_{ja}$ combines $c_q^A$ (kinetic), $\{c_q^P,\chi_0\}$ (mass) and $c_G$ (anomaly). Substitute $c_q^A=c_q^P+c_q^{AP}$ and $c_G=c_{GA}-\operatorname{tr}c_q^A$ and show the redundant combination ($\kappa$ of a constant axial redefinition) cancels in each shift. Proof by script: apply a symbolic constant redefinition (alp-sjp.1 transformation laws) and check invariance. The dependence of the meson shifts on $\operatorname{tr}\lambda_j c_q^{AP}$ and $\chi_0$ is then explicit. Alternative of leaving the results in terms of the bare $c_q^A$, $c_q^P$, $c_G$ rejected: the result would look gauge dependent.
4. **Pole alignment where it simplifies.** Aligned frame ($P=P'+w\,a'$ at $s=m_a^2$) kills the mixed block at that pole, so $m_a^2$ solves $D'_{aa}(m_a^2)=0$, $D'_{aa}=D_{aa}+2w\cdot D_{Pa}+w^{\mathsf T}D_{PP}w=D_{aa}-D_{aP}D_{PP}^{-1}D_{Pa}$ (Schur complement, same function as $D^{\rm eff}_{aa}$). Use it to read the ALP shift as one scalar equation and to split the kinetic, anomaly and exchange parts; meson poles use alignment at $s=m_{n}^2$ in the same way. Needed only for these decompositions, not for the expansions.
5. **Meson basis.** Isospin limit ($m_u=m_d$): $\pi^0=P_3$ (decoupled from $P_8,P_0$ by $M^2_{PP}$), $\eta$-$\eta'$ by the $2\times2$ rotation of $(P_8,P_0)$ in closed form with angle fixed by $\chi_0$ and $m_0^2$. General $m_q$: keep $m_{j,0}^2$ and the orthogonal $O$ as symbols, results written with projectors $\sum_{ij}O_{ji}\ldots$. Isospin-breaking $\pi^0$-$\eta$ mixing is carried by this symbolic $O$, with no further expansion. The $K^0$ doublet: with $\chi_0$ diagonal, the combination coupled to $a$ has norm$^2$ $|D_{6a}|^2+|D_{7a}|^2$ and shifts by the same formula with that norm; the orthogonal combination is exact. With a $d$-$s$ entry in $\chi_0$ the $K^0$ doublet mixes with $\pi^0,\eta,\eta'$ through $M^2_{PP}$ and is covered by the general-$O$ form.
6. **Validity.** Leading shifts are $O(\epsilon^2)$ with denominator $m_{j,0}^2-m_{a,0}^2$: level repulsion (meson heavier than ALP is pushed up). Near degeneracy use the exact $2\times2$ formula of the existing record; the expansion parameter is $|D_{ja}|/|m_{j,0}^2-m_{a,0}^2|$.
7. **Verification only by scripts; analytics delegated.** Each derivation is a SymPy script (coder agent). Numerical comparison uses exact rational sources with `solve_alp_meson_spectrum.py` and convergence exponents ($\epsilon^2$, $\epsilon^4$ residuals) as the *check* of the analytical formulas, never as the result. Notation follows Alda:2026nrd; new symbols only with approval: proposed $\delta m_j^2\equiv m_j^2-m_{j,0}^2$ and $\delta m_a^2\equiv m_a^2-m_{a,0}^2$ (clear physical meaning).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Expansion fails when $m_{a,0}$ is near a meson mass, the case of most interest for light ALPs] → state the domain, give the two-level formula, report the exact implicit equations as the all-order result.
- [Sign conventions of $v_i$, $c_G$ term] → the script reproduces the exact LO blocks first and compares with the solver before any expansion.
- [$K^0$ degeneracy and CP-violating $d$-$s$ phases] → treat via the norm $|D_{6a}|^2+|D_{7a}|^2$, check with the rephasing of the neutral-block record.
- [Isospin-breaking $\pi^0$-$\eta$ mixing makes closed forms heavy] → keep $O$ symbolic, give explicit isospin-limit forms, check that the limit $m_u\to m_d$ of the general form matches.
- [Quadratic ALP-source term $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}\chi_2$ is a free input] → carried as a single additive symbol; set to zero for the linear-spurion case of Alda:2026nrd.

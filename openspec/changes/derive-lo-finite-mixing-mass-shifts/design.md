# Design

## Context

LO blocks (neutral basis $(P_3,P_8,P_0,P_6,P_7,a)$, [finite-mixing record](../../../memory/alp/shared/finite-alp-meson-mixing.md)): $K_{ij}=\delta_{ij}$, $K_{ia}=-v_i$, $K_{aa}=1+v^2$, $v_i=\epsilon\operatorname{tr}(c_q^A\lambda_i)$, with $\epsilon=f_\pi/f_a$ used only as the coupling scale of the blocks; $M^2_{PP}$ independent of $f_a$; $M^2_{ia}$ and $M^2_{aa}$ as given there. Existing results used as inputs: [charged decoupling](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-charged-meson-decoupling.md), [neutral block reduction and exact $K^0$ pole](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-neutral-block-reduction.md), [single-pole alignment](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-single-pole-alignment.md), [general redefinition invariants](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-general-chiral-redefinition-invariants.md), [near-degeneracy](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-near-degeneracy-two-level.md). Sources linear in $a/f_a$ (the quadratic blocks depend on higher Taylor coefficients only through $\Delta M^2_{aa}=-\tfrac{f_\pi^2}{2f_a^2}\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}\chi_2$, see the [joint-vertex record](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-joint-vertex-expansion.md)); this term is carried as one additive symbol in the ALP entry. With $M=S+iP$, $S$, $P$ Hermitian and $\chi=2B_0M$, it equals $\Delta M^2_{aa}=-\dfrac{f_\pi^2}{f_a^2}B_0\operatorname{tr}S_2$, with $S_2=d^2S/dx^2|_0$ the undressed second derivative (not the dressed $\widehat S_2$); $\operatorname{tr}P_2$ drops out. The weak-mixing expansion of the [weak-mixing record](../../../memory/alp/shared/alp-lo-weak-mixing-expansion.md) is not an input: this change does not expand in $f_\pi/f_a$.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** exact pole equations for the pseudoscalar mesons and the ALP at finite mixing; exact sum rules; the canonical-frame expression through invariants; interlacing bounds; closed approximations for isolated poles with stated error control; exact explicit forms at diagonal $\chi_0$; checks against the solver.
**Non-Goals:** expansion in $f_\pi/f_a$ of any kind; the $\epsilon^2$ and $\epsilon^4$ shifts as results; the isospin-limit rotation; the kinetic, anomaly and exchange split of the ALP shift; decay constants, residues, mixing angles, other multiplets, NLO/loops, uncertainties, numerics (see proposal).

## Decisions

1. **Exact relations first.** $\sum m_n^2=\operatorname{tr}(K^{-1}M^2)$ holds because the poles are the eigenvalues of $K^{-1}M^2$. With $K^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1+vv^{\mathsf T}&v\\v^{\mathsf T}&1\end{pmatrix}$ the trace is $\operatorname{tr}M^2_{PP}+v^{\mathsf T}M^2_{PP}v+2v\cdot M^2_{Pa}+M^2_{aa}$. $\prod m_n^2=\det M^2$ since $\det K=1$.
2. **Exact poles from the canonical frame.** The field change $P_i=P_i'+\epsilon\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_ic_q^A)\,a$ makes the kinetic matrix the identity, so the poles are the eigenvalues of one real symmetric matrix $M^2_{\rm can}$. Its meson block is $M^2_{PP}$, its border is $-\epsilon\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_n\mathcal C)$, and its corner is $M^2_{\hat a\hat a,\rm can}$, written through $c_{GA}$ and $\operatorname{tr}\widehat S_2$. The Schur form $D^{\rm eff}_{aa}(s)$ is the secular equation of this matrix. Cauchy interlacing places each pole between consecutive unmixed masses.
3. **Invariant form.** $\mathcal C=-\tfrac12\{\chi_0,c_q^{AP}\}-\tfrac{m_0^2}{N_f}c_{GA}\mathbb 1$ is the only source combination that enters the mixing. Constant axial redefinitions leave $c_q^{AP}$, $c_{GA}$ and $\operatorname{tr}\widehat S_2$ unchanged, so the spectrum is invariant to all orders. Proof by script with the alp-sjp.1 transformation laws.
4. **Closed approximations for isolated poles.** For a meson pole far from the other unmixed masses, the exact equation $(s-m_{n,0}^2)[s-\widetilde M_n(s)]=\epsilon^2\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_n\mathcal C)^2$ is solved by freezing $\widetilde M_n$ at $s=m_{n,0}^2$, giving a two-level closed form with an exact error bound. For the ALP pole, the frozen form and a fixed-point iteration are given, with its convergence condition. Both are approximations under the far-apart assumption, labelled as such in the records.
5. **Explicit forms at diagonal $\chi_0$.** The secular polynomial and the per-meson residues for $\pi^0$, $\eta$, $\eta'$ and the $K^0$ doublet, with no isospin-limit rotation. With a $d$-$s$ entry in $\chi_0$ the five neutral states mix and the general-$m_q$ form of Decision 2 applies.
6. **Regimes.** Small mixing: no shift at order $f_\pi/f_a$ is kept or derived in this change. Large mixing: the finite shifts are the exact poles of Decision 2 or their closed approximations of Decision 4.
7. **Verification only by scripts.** Each derivation is a SymPy script (coder agent). Numerical comparison uses exact rational sources with `solve_alp_meson_spectrum.py`, and error tables against the spacing of the unmixed masses as the *check* of the approximations, never as the result. Notation follows Alda:2026nrd. Records write $\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_n\mathcal C)$ explicitly and use $\mathbb M_s$ and $\mathcal C$; no $\delta m$ symbols are introduced.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Isolated approximations fail when two unmixed masses lie within about $\epsilon|\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_n\mathcal C)|$ of each other, which can happen for a light ALP near a meson level] → state the domain, use the exact arrow-matrix eigenvalues there.
- [Sign conventions of $v_i$ and of the $c_G$ term] → the script reproduces the exact LO blocks first and compares with the solver before any closed form.
- [$K^0$ degeneracy and CP-violating $d$-$s$ phases] → treat via the exact $K^0$ pole and the norm $|D_{6a}|^2+|D_{7a}|^2$, check with the rephasing of the neutral-block record.
- [Quadratic ALP-source term $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}\chi_2$ is a free input] → carried as a single additive symbol; set to zero for the linear-spurion case of Alda:2026nrd.

# RChT $VPP$, $VPa$, and $\gamma Pa$ Interactions

## Scope, inputs, and assumptions

The literature inputs are the leading even-parity RChT Lagrangian in the antisymmetric-tensor representation [recorded here](rcht-basics.md),

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm LO}^{\rm even}
=\mathcal L_2^\chi+\mathcal L_{\rm kin}^V
+\frac{F_V}{2\sqrt2}\langle V_{\mu\nu}f_+^{\mu\nu}\rangle
+\frac{iG_V}{\sqrt2}\langle V_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu\rangle,
$$

and the one-ALP CCWZ expansion [recorded here](../../extracts/Alda:2026nrd/appendix-a-expansion.md) [@Ecker:1988te; @Alda:2026nrd]. The conversion between tensor and Proca representations retains the local terms required by field redefinitions [@Ecker:1989yg]. All interaction formulas below are derived by [`scripts/derive_rcht_gv_flavor_structures.py`](../../../scripts/derive_rcht_gv_flavor_structures.py).

Conventions and assumptions are:

- natural units and $U=u^2=\exp(i\sqrt2\Phi/F)$;
- $\Phi=\sum_i\phi_i\lambda_i$, with generic noncommuting pseudoscalar generators $\lambda_i$;
- the neutral tensor vectors that mix with the photon are $V_{\mu\nu}=\sum_kT_kV^k_{\mu\nu}$, with generic real orthonormal diagonal generators $T_k$ and $c_k=\langle QT_k\rangle$;
- $\ell_\mu=r_\mu=-eQA_\mu$, while the axial ALP source gives $u_\mu\supset2(\partial_\mu a)c_q^A/f_a$;
- terms are linear in the ALP and linear in the interaction current generated by the $G_V$ operator;
- no ideal vector mixing, common vector mass, flavor commutativity, or short-distance relation among $F$, $F_V$, and $G_V$ is imposed;
- exact electromagnetic factors refer only to the truncated tree-level quadratic photon-vector model. A complete $O(e^2)$ calculation would also require the corresponding loops and counterterms.

The $F$ convention differs from a convention with $U=\exp(i\Phi/f_\pi)$ by $F=f_\pi$ at leading order and $\Phi_{f_\pi}=\sqrt2\Phi$.

## Vielbein expansion

At the field orders required here,

$$
u_\mu
=-\frac{\sqrt2}{F}\partial_\mu\Phi
+2\frac{\partial_\mu a}{f_a}c_q^A
-\frac{\sqrt2ie}{F}A_\mu[Q,\Phi]
+O(\Phi^2,a\Phi,e\Phi^2).
$$

The term proportional to $c_q^V$ begins at $O(a\Phi)$, so its product with the mesonic vielbein first contributes to $Va\Phi^2$, not to $Va\Phi$.

Since $V_{\mu\nu}=-V_{\nu\mu}$,

$$
V_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu
=\frac12V_{\mu\nu}[u^\mu,u^\nu].
$$

This Lorentz antisymmetry produces the flavor commutators below without assuming that any generators commute.

## Canonical photon and massive-vector fields

Use the quantities established in [the quadratic diagonalization](rcht-photon-vector-diagonalization.md),

$$
g_k=\sqrt2eF_Vc_k,
\qquad
\epsilon_k=\frac{g_k}{M_k},
\qquad
Z=1+\boldsymbol\epsilon^T\boldsymbol\epsilon,
$$

$$
K_V=\mathbf1-\frac{\boldsymbol\epsilon\boldsymbol\epsilon^T}{Z},
\qquad
K_V^{1/2}=\mathbf1-
\frac{\boldsymbol\epsilon\boldsymbol\epsilon^T}
{\sqrt Z(\sqrt Z+1)}.
$$

Let $O$ diagonalize the canonically normalized massive-vector mass matrix as in that record, and define $H=K_V^{1/2}O$. To first order in any interaction current linear in the original tensor field, tensor elimination and exact quadratic diagonalization give

$$
\boxed{
V^k_{\mu\nu}
=\sum_A\frac{H_{kA}}{M_k}W^A_{\mu\nu}
+\frac{\epsilon_k}{M_k\sqrt Z}F_{\gamma\mu\nu}.
}
$$

The $W^A_\mu$ are canonical massive Proca eigenfields. Terms quadratic in the interaction current are additional local operators and are beyond this record's one-vector/one-photon vertex scope. In particular, the cross term between the $PP$ and $Pa$ currents is a local $aP^3$ interaction and must accompany Proca exchange if exact equivalence to the original antisymmetric-tensor formulation is required [@Ecker:1989yg]. Its flavor-resolved coefficient, sign, Mandelstam form, and identical-particle limits are derived by [`scripts/derive_a3p_vector_exchange_amplitude.py`](../../../scripts/derive_a3p_vector_exchange_amplitude.py) and recorded in [the vector-exchange amplitude memory](../../alp/three-pseudoscalar/a3p-vector-exchange-amplitude.md). Define the effective generic flavor matrices

$$
\mathcal T_A\equiv\sum_k\frac{H_{kA}}{M_k}T_k,
$$

$$
\mathcal T_\gamma\equiv
\frac1{\sqrt Z}\sum_k\frac{\epsilon_k}{M_k}T_k
=\frac{\sqrt2eF_V}{\sqrt Z}
\sum_k\frac{c_k}{M_k^2}T_k.
$$

The same diagonalization replaces the photon field appearing in the gauged Goldstone current according to

$$
A_\mu=\frac{A_{\gamma\mu}}{\sqrt Z}-\sum_A\rho_AW^A_\mu,
\qquad
\rho_A\equiv
\frac{(\boldsymbol\epsilon^TK_V^{-1/2}O)_A}{Z}
=\frac{(\boldsymbol\epsilon^TO)_A}{\sqrt Z}.
$$

For nondegenerate starting masses and the continuous choice $O\to\mathbf1$ as $e\to0$, $H_{kA}=\delta_{kA}+O(e^2)$, while $\rho_A=O(e)$ and $1/\sqrt Z=1+O(e^2)$. The statement about $H$ is basis dependent inside a degenerate subspace. Charged vectors and neutral directions with $c_k=0$ do not mix with the photon; their tensor-origin $VPP$ and $VPa$ formulas follow by using their own generic generator divided by the corresponding tensor-to-Proca mass.

## One vector and two pseudoscalars

For two distinct component fields, with each unordered pair $i<j$ counted once, the $G_V$ operator gives

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{WPP}^{(G_V)}
=\frac{\sqrt2iG_V}{F^2}
\sum_A\sum_{i<j}W^A_{\mu\nu}
(\partial^\mu\phi_i)(\partial^\nu\phi_j)
\left\langle\mathcal T_A[\lambda_i,\lambda_j]\right\rangle .
}
$$

The gauged Goldstone kinetic term contains

$$
\mathcal L_{A_{\rm old}PP}^{\chi}
=ieA_{{\rm old}\mu}\left\langle Q[\Phi,\partial^\mu\Phi]\right\rangle.
$$

Its massive-vector component is therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{WPP}^{(\chi)}
=-ie\sum_A\sum_{i<j}\rho_AW^A_\mu
\left(\phi_i\partial^\mu\phi_j-\phi_j\partial^\mu\phi_i\right)
\left\langle Q[\lambda_i,\lambda_j]\right\rangle .
}
$$

Thus $\mathcal L_{WPP}=\mathcal L_{WPP}^{(G_V)}+\mathcal L_{WPP}^{(\chi)}$ within the exactly transformed truncated tree-level Lagrangian. The second term begins at $O(e^2)$ because $\rho_A=O(e)$; it is absent when consistently retaining only one electromagnetic insertion.

The term with two identical pseudoscalar components vanishes because $[\lambda_i,\lambda_i]=0$. Before conversion to canonical Proca fields, replace $\mathcal T_AW^A_{\mu\nu}$ by $T_AV^A_{\mu\nu}$ and omit the inverse-mass factor implicit in $\mathcal T_A$.

## One vector, one pseudoscalar, and one ALP

For a generic pseudoscalar component, the $G_V$ operator gives

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{WPa}^{(G_V)}
=-\frac{2iG_V}{Ff_a}
\sum_{A,i}W^A_{\mu\nu}
(\partial^\mu\phi_i)(\partial^\nu a)
\left\langle\mathcal T_A[\lambda_i,c_q^A]\right\rangle .
}
$$

The massive-vector component of the directly gauged Goldstone term gives

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{WPa}^{(\chi)}
=-\frac{\sqrt2ieF}{f_a}
\sum_{A,i}\rho_AW^A_\mu(\partial^\mu a)\phi_i
\left\langle[Q,c_q^A]\lambda_i\right\rangle .
}
$$

Hence $\mathcal L_{WPa}=\mathcal L_{WPa}^{(G_V)}+\mathcal L_{WPa}^{(\chi)}$ in the exactly transformed truncated Lagrangian. The Goldstone-current contribution is $O(e^2)$ and vanishes under the standard assumption $[Q,c_q^A]=0$.

The tensor-origin interaction vanishes for a channel in which the relevant pseudoscalar generator commutes with the axial ALP spurion. In the no-ALP-meson-mixing parametrization of [the LO ALP derivation](../../alp/three-pseudoscalar/alp-phi3-lo-chipt-derivation.md), one may substitute $c_q^A\to-\mathbb M_a^{-1}(C)$.

## One photon, one pseudoscalar, and one ALP

There are two structurally different terms.

### Mixing-induced RChT term

The photon component of the original tensor field induces

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\gamma Pa}^{\rm mix}
=-\frac{2iG_V}{Ff_a}
\sum_iF_{\gamma\mu\nu}
(\partial^\mu\phi_i)(\partial^\nu a)
\left\langle\mathcal T_\gamma[\lambda_i,c_q^A]\right\rangle .
}
$$

At leading electromagnetic order this is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\gamma Pa}^{\rm mix}
=-\frac{2\sqrt2ieF_VG_V}{Ff_a}
\sum_{i,k}\frac{c_k}{M_k^2}
F_{\gamma\mu\nu}
(\partial^\mu\phi_i)(\partial^\nu a)
\left\langle T_k[\lambda_i,c_q^A]\right\rangle
+O(e^3).
}
$$

This term is separately photon-gauge invariant. The condition $[Q,c_q^A]=0$ does not force it to vanish when the nondegenerate vector masses make $\sum_kc_kT_k/M_k^2$ different from a multiple of $Q$. In the degenerate complete-basis limit $M_k=M_V$, $\sum_kc_kT_k=Q$, and it does vanish for $[Q,c_q^A]=0$ by trace cyclicity.

### Direct Goldstone term

The gauged Goldstone kinetic Lagrangian also gives

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\gamma Pa}^{\rm direct}
=\frac{\sqrt2ieF}{f_a\sqrt Z}
\sum_iA_{\gamma\mu}(\partial^\mu a)\phi_i
\left\langle[Q,c_q^A]\lambda_i\right\rangle .
}
$$

For the standard electromagnetically invariant ALP spurion assumption,

$$
[Q,c_q^A]=0,
$$

this direct term vanishes. If that commutator is nonzero, the spurion's electromagnetic transformation must be specified before treating the displayed contact term as a standalone physical interaction.

## Role of the $F_V$ operator and evidence status

At the requested field orders, the $F_V$ operator supplies the quadratic neutral-vector--photon transition. For a purely electromagnetic vector source, the term in $f_+^{\mu\nu}$ linear in $\Phi$ cancels, so $F_V$ does not directly generate $VPP$ or $VPa$. Its appearance in $\mathcal L_{\gamma Pa}^{\rm mix}$ is indirect, through photon-vector diagonalization.

- **Literature inputs:** the RChT operators and tensor conventions [@Ecker:1988te], the need for induced local terms under spin-one field redefinitions [@Ecker:1989yg], and the ALP vielbein expansion [@Alda:2026nrd].
- **Derived results:** the displayed tensor-origin and Goldstone-current interaction terms, the effective flavor matrices $\mathcal T_A$ and $\mathcal T_\gamma$, and the direct-versus-mixing-induced separation.
- **Explicit approximation:** exact formulas resum only the truncated tree-level quadratic mixing; the leading-$e$ expression retains one electromagnetic mixing insertion.

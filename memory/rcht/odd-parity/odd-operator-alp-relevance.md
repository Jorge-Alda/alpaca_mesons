# Target relevance

The 228 independent action operators, including 217 additions, are not an
observable or fit-parameter count. Source restrictions, flavour traces and
canonical external tensor contractions can identify combinations or kill
otherwise independent action operators.

- VJP derivative operators, including one $X=\chi_+$ insertion, enter direct
  $a\to V\gamma$ and single-vector response in $a\to\gamma\gamma$.
- VVP derivative operators, including one $X$ insertion, enter direct
  $a\to VV$, one vector-to-photon response in $a\to V\gamma$, and two
  responses in $a\to\gamma\gamma$.
- $Y=i\chi_-$ mass/source operators and their two-derivative extensions
  carry ALP source/mass information independent of the derivative weight.
- Single/double traces and singlet structures change neutral-channel flavour
  sensitivity, especially singlet-sensitive channels. A photon in a singleton
  trace vanishes because $\operatorname{tr}Q=0$; mixed traces need not vanish.
- $f_-$ operators need their own source expansion. Treating $f_-$ as an
  ordinary independent photon field misses its scalar-photon insertion.
- Nonlinear covariant remainders require field-order projection before their
  relevance is inferred from the action count.

The checked single-odd-insertion tree $a\to3P$ amplitude is zero: four scalar
external particles supply only three independent momenta for an epsilon
contraction. The chiral/even-vector sector supplies the retained contribution.
This statement is scoped to that amplitude and accuracy, not arbitrary
multi-particle or multi-odd-insertion amplitudes.

The implemented leading scalar electromagnetic kernel has rank 23 versus
baseline rank 8, hence 15 additional formal kernel directions. This is neither
a complete independent-source ALP rank nor a measurement identifiability rank.

# Which mismatch can the extension address?

For the flavour-source obstruction $S\propto Q$ under universal odd
interactions, the primary candidates are the one-$\chi_+$ derivative VJP/VVP
single- and double-trace structures. They supply additional channel-dependent
interaction tensors. Singlet structures supply further neutral flavour freedom.
Their sufficiency requires solving the enlarged hard-expansion equations;
constant cancellation alone does not establish the correct nonzero leading
coefficient. Ordinary mass double traces alone cannot supply the missing
leading double-virtual constant cancellation.

For the chiral common-pole correlator/TFF incompatibility, $\chi_+$ breaking
vanishes in the strict chiral limit, and singlet structures absent in the
traceless pion projection cannot help there. Independent pseudoscalar-source
variation of $\chi_-$ can modify a correlator even when the corresponding
on-shell chiral-pion insertion vanishes. This has not established simultaneous
TFF/OPE matching. Extra vector poles or pseudoscalar-resonance dynamics remain
candidate extensions; their compatibility examples are documented in the
linked mismatch record, not verified completions of this action.

Low-energy calibration tests the enlarged action within its stated domain;
it does not repair a hard-limit mismatch by itself. The next calculation is
an amplitude projection retaining independent derivative and mass ALP weights,
followed by physical flavour restrictions and normalized response/contact
assembly. No operator is discarded solely to force a desired hard cancellation.

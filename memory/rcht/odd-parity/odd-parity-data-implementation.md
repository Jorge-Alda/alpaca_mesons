# Conventions, authorization, and inputs

Natural units and the non-HLS, one-multiplet odd-parity RChT conventions recorded in the [omega predictor](omega-pi-gamma-rcht-predictor.md) are used. The user explicitly authorizes a diagonal Gaussian approximation to published errors when no covariance is available. Symmetric errors use their quoted diagonal values; BaBar Table 11 retains its published asymmetric lower/upper magnitudes and defines its eventual diagonal standard deviation as their mean absolute magnitude, $(\sigma_-+\sigma_+)/2$. Identified source-wide normalization, luminosity, or known correlated systematic errors are excluded from diagonal errors and represented as explicit standardized nuisances. These are likelihood assumptions, not literature results.

[`odd_parity_data.py`](../../../scripts/implementation/odd_parity_data.py) takes these inputs:

1. literal extracted CSV and versioned JSON sidecars generated by [`extract_odd_parity_tables.py`](../../../scripts/extract_odd_parity_tables.py), with literature provenance in the corresponding `memory/extracts/` records;
2. caller-supplied [`OmegaPiGammaInputs`](../../../scripts/implementation/radiative_odd_parity.py) and positive total omega width `GammaOmegaTotal`, all masses and widths in GeV;
3. caller-supplied [`OPEParameters`](../../../scripts/implementation/odd_parity_rcht.py), truncation fraction and equicorrelation for the [fixed OPE prior](correlated-odd-parity-fit-infrastructure.md);
4. caller-supplied starts and bounds for the canonical ordering $(c_1,\ldots,c_7,d_1,\ldots,d_4)$.

# Deterministic method and outputs

Each loader first verifies both the CSV and sidecar SHA256, then validates the exact CSV schema, finite represented numbers, declared units, and the complete `odd-parity-likelihood-v1` object. Returned NumPy structured arrays are immutable. Missing KLOE fit entries remain empty strings rather than being converted to nonfinite sentinels. Statistical and systematic columns remain separate, BaBar Table 11 asymmetric errors remain positive lower/upper magnitudes, and BaBar $3\pi$ retains the $N_{corr}$ and efficiency error columns.

Its provenance source ID `pdg-2024-omega-pi0-gamma-total` remains recorded in the checksum-verified sidecar metadata rather than in the fit object, whose API carries no source-ID bookkeeping. The fixed-OPE adapter combines `ope_linear_system` and `fixed_ope_prior` without changing the canonical coupling ordering. The OPE matrix has rank five and leaves rank deficiency six in the eleven-dimensional parameter space. The connected branching datum does not justify a global-fit claim; no physical fit has been run.

# Remaining readiness and verification

The CLEO and BaBar transition-form-factor tables, BaBar three-pion spectrum, and KLOE fit table have validated loaders only. They deliberately do not create `Observation` objects because their physical predictors are absent. The BaBar sidecars record unconnected multiplicative source IDs and fractions: $3.0\%$ cross-section normalization, $2.3\%$ form-factor normalization, and $1.2\%$ luminosity normalization. The BaBar three-pion sidecar also records its known fully correlated mass-bin systematic as one unconnected additive nuisance with shift vector `sigma_syst_nb` in nb; only its statistical error is diagonal. Other radiative channels also remain blocked by flavor-resolved matching. HLS is excluded.

Verification commands are:

```text
.venv/bin/python scripts/extract_odd_parity_tables.py
.venv/bin/python -m unittest scripts.implementation.test_odd_parity_data
.venv/bin/python -m unittest scripts.implementation.test_correlated_fit
.venv/bin/python -m unittest scripts.implementation.test_radiative_odd_parity
```

The loader tests check row counts, immutability, asymmetric-error preservation, exact nuisance metadata, connected omega variance/configuration, canonical OPE adaptation, exposed rank deficiency, and checksum/nonfinite/unit rejection. Regenerating the extraction gives exactly the recorded hashes above.

# Physical aVgamma completion plan

## Starting evidence

The [direct VJP derivation](odd-parity-coupling-prescriptions-and-direct-alp-vgamma.md)
provides ALP source insertion coefficients and proves the direct on-shell
$c_7$ zero. The [numerical kernel](odd-parity-rcht-numerical-kernel.md) stores
uncontracted VJP/VVP coefficients; it is not the full physical $a\to V\gamma$
amplitude. The [fresh projected audit](alp-two-boson-blind-direction-audit.md)
also removes $d_4$ and the common $c_2,c_5,c_6$ shift in the retained wedge
contractions. Complete normalization/contact assembly remains unperformed.

## Inputs and truncation

Use Alda arXiv-v1 independent $X=\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C)$ and
$Y=c_q^{AP}+X$, the VJP/VVP operator basis, and the
[calibrated stage-4 response](vector-stage4-amplitudes.md).
Assume tree level, one ALP, electromagnetic-compatible sources and the retained
single-trace action at leading photon order. Preserve the operator scale $M_V$
separately from the external vector mass and each internal pole mass.
Keep both vector branches and explicit source-sign hypotheses; do not select
a sign from an electronic width. Extra odd operators and higher orders are
separate model choices.

## Ordered calculation

1. In `scripts/derive_physical_alp_vgamma.py`, contract every direct VJP operator
   with the physical ALP derivative and mass insertions, the physical external
   vector tensor, and the real photon field strength. Recheck $f_-=0$ from
   the source assumptions rather than silently carrying a SM-pion expansion
   into this calculation. Do not set $Y=X$.
2. Contract the VVP terms with both vector assignments, replacing the photon
   leg by the signed complete $W(0)$ response. The external vector gets
   $1/M_{\rm ext}$; the internal response gets its action-derived inverse
   mass matrix. The explicit photon source remains $Q$.
3. Assemble any retained WZW/counterterm, ALP–meson LSZ and field-redefinition
   contact terms in one convention. Compare with Alda's no-mixing variables
   and avoid counting both an absorbed meson pole and its original graph.
   Gauge/anomaly normalization does not determine independent finite resonance
   or local matching coefficients.
4. Derive the complete coefficient of
   $\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}\varepsilon_V^\alpha
   \varepsilon_\gamma^\beta q_V^\rho q_\gamma^\sigma$, with all factors
   of $e,f_a,M_V,M_{\rm ext}$ and relative signs generated by the script.
   Verify the photon Ward identity, field reparametrization, vector phase,
   and independent-spurion limits.
5. Derive the spin-zero crossed-decay width and threshold behavior. For an
   unstable/broad daughter vector, define the daughter-channel observable and
   its off-shell treatment before claiming an inclusive prediction.
6. Project the completed coupling gradient onto all current blind columns;
   repeat over both mass branches and the source hypotheses. The existing
   stripped zero is a benchmark, not permission to omit a required contact.

## Deliverables

Save a paired executable/memory derivation and a fit-ready numerical predictor
with independent Ward, field-basis, normalization and null-shift tests. State
UV ALP inputs, any added local coefficients and the pointwise Alda mixing-validity
domain. Keep physical coupling uncertainty distinct from the structural
row-span check and from daughter line-shape/model uncertainty.

# Observable and unified coefficient coordinates

This is one likelihood for the inclusive BaBar Tables 2 and 3:
$e^+e^-\to K_S K^\pm\pi^\mp$ and $e^+e^-\to K^+K^-\pi^0$,
through the $1.76$--$1.78\,\mathrm{GeV}$ bin. It uses the full coherent
electromagnetic form factors $F_a+F_b+F_c+F_d$ of [@Qin:2024ulb], including
WZW, VPPP, VJP, VVP, two excited-vector multiplets, and the open
$a_2(1320)$ contribution in the $K_S K^\pm\pi^\mp$ channel. The two total
cross sections are integrated over their Dalitz phase spaces. The
charge-conjugate $K_S K^\pm\pi^\mp$ modes are summed with the source factor
of two. BaBar Table 7 is an isobar component from the same events and is not
included as a separate likelihood block.

The fit varies the ten combinations that appear in the full source
form factors:
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_A&=c_1-c_2+c_5,&
c_B&=c_1-c_2-c_5+2c_6,\\
c_M&=c_1+c_2+8c_3-c_5,&
c_4,&\\
d_M&=d_1+8d_2-d_3,&
d_3,&\\
g_A&=g_1+2g_2-g_3,&
g_2,&
g_4,&
g_M&=2g_4+g_5.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fit uses these coordinates directly. A displayed individual
$(c_i,d_i,g_i)$ vector is one arbitrary representative of the coefficient
image and does not assign measured values to its null coordinates.

# Inputs and assumptions

The source-transcribed [Table 2 data](../../../data/vector_sector/babar_2007_kskpi_inclusive_below_1p78.csv)
and [Table 3 data](../../../data/vector_sector/babar_2007_kkpi0_inclusive_below_1p78.csv)
are regenerated by
[extract_babar_kkpi_inclusive_tables.py](../../../scripts/extract_babar_kkpi_inclusive_tables.py).
The likelihood uses their statistical errors as diagonal. The reported
inclusive cross-section systematics, 5.1% for Table 2 and 6.5% for Table 3,
enter as separate common normalization nuisances. The +4.2% and +4.6%
corrections are already applied to the tabulated values. The extra 15%
systematic belongs to the Table 7 Dalitz isobar extraction and is not
assigned to these inclusive rates [@BaBar:2007ceh].

The form factors use averaged kaon/pion masses at vertices and physical
masses in phase space, ideal neutral-vector mixing, the current preliminary
values of $F_V,G_V,z_V$, two real excited-vector residues per channel, and
two $a_2$ tensor products as nuisance parameters. The $K_2^*$ pair pole is
omitted in this window. The preliminary odd coefficients provide starting
values only; they are not fixed and are not added as a second likelihood or
a coefficient prior. No OPE relation or prior on the ten odd coefficients
is imposed.

For comparison, a previous preliminary-fixed $c_i,d_i$ fit had
$\chi^2=427.86/39$ and is rejected as a model description. OPE-constrained
g-only fits are retained in separate diagnostic outputs but are not used as
the unified result or as priors on these coefficients.

# Fit and coefficient information

The joint fit converges with
$$
\chi^2=35.7164,\qquad \mathrm{dof}=29,\qquad p=0.182.
$$
There are no active coefficient bounds. The two channel contributions are
$18.726$ and $16.991$ to the statistical chi-square. The fitted
normalization pulls are negligible. The local coefficient coordinates and
linearized errors are:

| Combination | Fit value | Local error |
|---|---:|---:|
| $c_A$ | $0.0486$ | $0.0894$ |
| $c_B$ | $0.1686$ | $1.7602$ |
| $c_M$ | $0.00004$ | $4.8858$ |
| $c_4$ | $-0.0644$ | $0.3342$ |
| $d_M$ | $-1.5051$ | $14.7563$ |
| $d_3$ | $0.5628$ | $2.7009$ |
| $g_A$ | $-0.0758$ | $4.2828$ |
| $g_2$ | $-0.0666$ | $1.6885$ |
| $g_4$ | $-0.0960$ | $2.1601$ |
| $g_M$ | $-0.4940$ | $1.6761$ |

The profiled integrated-rate Jacobian has rank ten at relative singular-value
thresholds from $10^{-6}$ through $10^{-10}$. An independent exact rational
amplitude calculation gives rank ten for both the ground-pole model and the
two-excited-vector model. Finite widths do not lift an amplitude null in this
source setup. The fit therefore has no additional local coefficient null
among the ten reported combinations.

The exact six-dimensional null in the original 16 coefficients is generated
by $c_7$, $d_4$, $\delta g_1=\delta g_3$,
$\delta d_1=-8\delta d_2$, the common shift
$\delta c_2=\delta c_5=\delta c_6$, and
$\delta c_1=\delta c_2=t,\ \delta c_3=-t/4$.

The $g$-combination profile scans do not yield certified confidence
intervals. Fixing each coordinate to zero costs only
$\Delta\chi^2=0.0141$ for $g_A$, $0.1823$ for $g_2$, $0.1209$ for $g_4$,
and $0.7498$ for $g_M$, while outer scan points have optimizer failures or
active nuisance bounds. Thus the unified fit has nonzero local sensitivity
to the VPPP combinations, but the BaBar rates alone do not establish a
nonzero value for any one of them at one standard deviation.

# Reproduction and stored diagnostics

Recreate the tables and run:

    .venv/bin/python scripts/extract_babar_kkpi_inclusive_tables.py
    .venv/bin/python scripts/fit_babar_kkpi_unified_vector_coefficients.py --output data/vector_sector/babar_kkpi_unified_vector_coefficients_fit.json

The [fit script](../../../scripts/fit_babar_kkpi_unified_vector_coefficients.py)
uses analytic derivatives for the same coherent bin likelihood. Its
[machine-readable output](../../../data/vector_sector/babar_kkpi_unified_vector_coefficients_fit.json)
stores coefficient coordinates, arbitrary original-basis representative,
null certificate, zero-width and finite-width amplitude ranks, integrated
rate singular values, profiles with convergence flags, channel predictions,
and quadrature refinement. The global nonlinear minimum is not proven.

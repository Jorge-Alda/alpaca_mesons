# Fit objective

The proposed fit determines only the two combinations
$g_1-g_2-g_3$ and $2g_4+g_5$ that enter the isoscalar charged-three-pion
local vertex. It does not fit five independent $g_i$. The executable
[local projection](isoscalar-vppp-three-pion-projection.md) and its
[photon-source response](isoscalar-vppp-photon-response.md) establish the
coefficient map. The conditional $2g_4+g_5$ number in [@GomezDumm:2010ra]
must not be reused as independent data: it is inferred from the same on-shell
$\omega$ rate after that paper imposes its short-distance relations and
$G_V=F_V/F^2$.

The local term must enter a complete complex Khuri--Treiman amplitude for
$\gamma^*(q^2)\to3\pi$, together with the WZW normalization, vector exchange,
vector-source residues, and crossed-channel rescattering. Use one declared
vector self-energy and local-source prescription. The bare photon response
is in [the source-response record](isoscalar-vppp-photon-response.md); it is
not itself a physical timelike line shape.

In the dispersive convention, $a(q^2)$ is a subtraction function multiplying
the Khuri--Treiman solution, not the full physical P wave. The 2025 global fit
parameterizes it with an anomaly subtraction $\alpha_A=C_A/3$, vector
resonance terms, and an inelastic conformal polynomial. The cited analyses
do not supply an RChT $g_i$-to-$a(q^2)$ map. For this fit preparation we now
select an explicit conditional prescription: match the Dalitz-independent
local full scalar to one third of the subtraction seed at $q^2=0$, then
propagate that seed through the Khuri--Treiman solution. The resulting shifts
to $\alpha_A$ and the bare vector residues, and the associated sum-rule
mismatch, are derived in the
[conditional matching record](isoscalar-vppp-dispersive-matching.md).

# Required parameterization and identifiability checks

Fit the two literal combinations jointly with declared resonance/source and
dispersive nuisance parameters. Under the conditional mapping, the pieces of
the fitted anomaly subtraction and vector-pole residues generated by these
local terms must be replaced or constrained by the combinations; do not float
the same constant and pole residue independently on top of them. The local
one-pole term is exactly a constant plus a pole, and an independent constant
removes one profiled rank. Tie the effective direct normalization to the
mapped WZW plus VPPP source coefficient, or include an explicit external
constraint. Retain the conformal/inelastic term needed to satisfy the
published spectral sum rule, then check the nuisance-profiled whitened
Jacobian rank and singular values. Show profiles for each
combination, with and without theory priors. Repeat for conformal-polynomial
order, phase continuation, resonance content, and energy cuts. A rank-two
polynomial at two energies is necessary but not sufficient for
identifiability in the full amplitude.

The likelihood should compare measured $e^+e^-\to3\pi$ cross sections with
bin-integrated phase-space predictions from the complex dispersive amplitude.
Use published statistical covariance and represent known common luminosity
and systematic uncertainties as shared nuisance parameters with their stated
Gaussian constraints. If a covariance is unpublished, use only a clearly
declared diagonal approximation for the eligible errors, while keeping known
correlated terms out of that diagonal. Cross sections measure the modulus;
the phase information is supplied conditionally by the declared dispersive
model. Do not describe that phase as independently measured.

# Present data and blockers

- The cached [BaBar 2004 table](../../../data/odd_parity/babar_3pi_born_spectrum.csv)
  contains 78 bins from $\sqrt{s}=1.0625$ to $2.9875$ GeV and misses the
  $\omega$ and $\phi$ peaks.
- The source-backed [BaBar 2021 extraction](babar-2021-three-pion-fit-input.md)
  supplies 114 bins with statistical and residual-systematic covariance from
  0.62 to 1.10 GeV, plus 72 bins from 1.10 to 3.50 GeV. It is a usable
  measurement input, but its dispersive response and a complete cross-section
  prediction are still missing.
- The held [KLOE $\phi\to3\pi$ table](kloe-phi-three-pion-fit-blocker.md)
  contains a fit summary rather than reusable Dalitz bins. Its tree-level
  amplitude decomposition is not an appropriate replacement for the
  rescattered amplitude.
- [Hoferichter:2025lcz] updates the global SND, CMD-2, and BaBar analysis and
  provides normalization/model variants, but not a complex $f_1(s,q^2)$ grid
  or complete correlated parameter replicas. The repository's source audit
  records these limits and the needed reconstruction steps
  [here](pion-tff-spectral-input-source-audit.md).
- The central complex pion vector form factor grid exists, but the correlated
  phase and fit variations needed for joint TFF uncertainty propagation do
  not.

Therefore the repository now has one covariance-backed ground-state
cross-section input, but still lacks a validated finite-width Khuri--Treiman
response and the corresponding full likelihood. Defensible numerical
estimates for either combination require that response, declared source and
nonlocal-residue inputs, and additional SND/CMD-2 data or a justified
single-experiment fit scope. Only then can fitted amplitude replicas be used
in the existing pion-TFF spectral input and moment calculation.

# Fit preparation status

The fit is prepared at the operator/source level with the BaBar 2021 data
connected as a verified measurement input. No measurement likelihood or
numerical parameter estimate is claimed. The fit should not proceed by
fitting only the cached older BaBar bins or by reading central values
from published marginal parameter errors as if they were correlated samples.

# Selection of the one-loop $\sigma\to\pi^+\pi^-$ graphs for arbitrary $\epsilon$

This record gives the rules that decide which graphs enter the estimate in the OpenSpec change `estimate-sigma-pipi-one-loop-murayama` (tasks 2.1, 2.3 and 4.4, decisions D3, D4, D10, D11, D12). The rules apply for any $\epsilon=f_\pi/f_a$. The regime of interest is $\epsilon\gg1$, and no step assumes $\epsilon\ll1$. The resulting list is in the [graph-list record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-list.md), and the large-$\epsilon$ behaviour of the pole data is in the [large-$\epsilon$ record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-scaling.md). No loop is evaluated.

## Inputs

- Graph enumeration, symmetry factors, internal lines: [graph-list record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-list.md). Vertices: [vertex-rule record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-vertex-rules.md). Legs, tree $M_0=-\frac{B_0\epsilon}{2f_\pi}\langle\widehat S_{1,0}\{\lambda_{\pi^+},\lambda_{\pi^-}\}\rangle$ and assumptions: [tree and legs record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-tree-and-legs.md).
- Pole data $v_a$, $N(m_a^2)$, $\mathbb M^{-1}_{m_a^2}(\mathcal C)$ and the meson-line residue: [large-$\epsilon$ record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-scaling.md).

## Rules

1. **Every graph that does not vanish identically is kept.** This includes the one-loop 1PI tadpoles, bubbles and triangles and the self-energies of the external legs. It includes the graphs with both a radial and an ALP-like line. No graph and no term is dropped by a series in $\epsilon$ or in $1/\epsilon$. "Vanishes identically" means $T_\Gamma=0$ for every routing of the loop momentum, by the charge rule or the assumptions. For a self-energy whose $\delta Z$ is exactly zero (a tadpole whose vertex does not depend on $p$), the graph only shifts the mass, and the physical masses are used.

2. **Large-$\epsilon$ behaviour (orientation only).** The leading powers of $\epsilon$ of every kept graph at large $\epsilon$, with fixed sources and fixed $\lambda$, are in the [large-$\epsilon$ graph record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-graphs.md). They do not decide whether a graph is kept.

3. **D4 as an exact split (restated criterion).** The residue of each internal meson line is written as $(\mathbf 1_9\oplus1)-v_av_a^{\rm T}$, i.e. three pieces: the bare $U(3)$ generator sum, the ALP-field entry, and minus the ALP-like pole. One term is excluded. It is the term in which every internal line is a meson line taking the bare generator sum, and in which the only power of $\epsilon$ is the single $\sigma$ end, as in the tree. With the scales of rule 5 this term has the scale $m_P$. The criterion, checked by the script on every routing, is: the term contains no letter of the ALP sector ($\mathbb M^{-1}_{m_a^2}(\mathcal C)$, $N(m_a^2)$, $\widehat P_{0,1}$, $\widehat S_{r,s\ge1}$, $\widehat P_{r,s\ge1}$), and its ratio to $M_0$ (its $\delta Z$ for a $\pi^\pm$ leg) depends on $\epsilon$ only through the kinematics $p_\sigma^2=m_\sigma^2$. This term is the chiral background of the model without the ALP. It is recorded but not estimated, and it matters only if it alone approaches $R_{\max}$. Everything else is kept: the ALP-field-entry pieces, the $-v_av_a^{\rm T}$ pieces, and the graphs with more than one $\sigma$ end or ALP-field end. For example, the meson-only $\sigma$ self-energy has two $\sigma$ ends, so its all-generator term is kept. The large-$\epsilon$ limit of the projector is not used in the results.

4. **D11: CP and flavour (assumption).** The source terms are assumed CP-invariant, with $\sigma$ CP-even and $a$, $\Phi$ CP-odd ($\Phi\to-\Phi^{\rm T}$). Invariance of $\langle\widehat S_{r,s}\cos\frac{\Phi}{f_\pi}\rangle$ and $\langle\widehat P_{r,s}\sin\frac{\Phi}{f_\pi}\rangle$ under $a\to-a$, $\Phi\to-\Phi^{\rm T}$ requires $\widehat S_{r,s}=(-1)^s\widehat S_{r,s}^{\rm T}$ and $\widehat P_{r,s}=(-1)^{s+1}\widehat P_{r,s}^{\rm T}$. Hence $\langle\widehat S_{r,s}\rangle=0$ for odd $s$, in particular $\langle\widehat S_{1,1}\rangle=0$, and $\sigma$ cannot mix with the ALP or with any neutral pseudoscalar at any loop order. Mixing of $\pi^+$ with another charged pole ($K^+$) vanishes if the sources and the quark mass matrix are flavour-diagonal (assumption; this implies that the sources commute with $Q$). These conditions are not imposed as matrix identities in the trace algebra, which stays generic and noncommuting. They only remove the one-particle-reducible graphs in which an external leg mixes at one loop with another state. Those graphs are not enumerated.

5. **Loop scale, loop momentum and routing (D3, D12; assumptions).**
   - Every term of a graph (one choice of piece on each meson line) has its own scale $m_{\rm term}$, the largest of the scales of its lines: $m_\sigma$ for a radial line; $m_a$ for an ALP-like line; for a meson line, $m_P$ for the bare generator sum (piece (i)) and $m_a$ for the rest, pieces (ii)+(iii)+(iv) $=(0\oplus1)-v_av_a^{\rm T}$ (ALP-field entry $(N-1)/N$, mixed entries, meson block of the ALP-like pole), which is the remainder of the ALP-like propagator. $m_P$ is one symbolic mass, the largest pole mass of the nine meson-like states; a mass per generator is not possible because the completeness sum runs over all nine generators on every bare line. Bare meson lines also enter the maximum in graphs with a radial or ALP-like line (e.g. $\max(m_P,m_\sigma)$). No lower limit on $m_{\rm term}$ other than the line scales is imposed.
   - Consequence: with a common scale $m_a$ and a common routing, an ALP-like line plus pieces (ii)+(iii)+(iv) of a meson line equals the bare ALP-field line (identity of the integrands, tested), so the internal ALP–meson mass mixing is neglected inside the loops (it is kept in the vertices and on the external legs), and the factor $m_a^2-m_\sigma^2$ of $\delta Z_{\pi^\pm}$ disappears.
   - In each term, products with the loop momentum, $k\cdot k$ and $k\cdot p$, are set to $m_{\rm term}^2$, and the size factor is $S_\Gamma(m_{\rm term}^2)^{2-r_\Gamma}/(16\pi^2)$.
   - Routing: for each of the $2r_\Gamma$ routings (loop momentum on each internal line, both orientations) the graph value is $\sum_tC_t^\rho t$ over the independent products of traces $t$; the graph enters with $\sum_tC_t^{\rho_t^*}t$, $\rho_t^*$ the routing of largest $|C_t^\rho|$, sign kept (termwise maximum). This is an upper bound per graph on the routing ambiguity of the size rule, not on the loop: $|\sum_tC_t^\rho t|\le\sum_t|C_t^{\rho_t^*}||t|$ for every $\rho$. Ties of equal magnitude and opposite sign take the sign of the sum of the coefficients over all routings; if this sum vanishes, the first routing in the canonical order is used and the product is marked. Where $\rho_t^*$ depends on the masses, the branches are inequalities between squared masses (Piecewise coefficients); at large $\epsilon$ the choice is made by the leading power in $\epsilon$, then by the size of the leading coefficient. The routing spread is kept as a diagnostic.
   - External-leg factor: $\delta Z=-\Pi'(m^2)$, with the derivative acting on the explicit $p^2$ dependence of the numerator and, for $r_\Gamma\ge2$, on one propagator (one more power of $1/m_{\rm term}^2$), term by term and in each routing.
   - Tadpoles (one-point functions): under CP the tadpoles of $\pi^0$, $\eta$, $\eta'$ and of the ALP field vanish exactly. The $\sigma$ tadpole is set to zero by assumption: it is a vacuum condition on $f_a$, consistent with eliminating $\lambda$ through $m_\sigma^2$.

## Consequences

- 48 1PI graphs: 26 kept, 22 vanish identically. With the external legs, 6 $\sigma$ self-energies and 5 for each of $\pi^\pm$ are kept, 42 graphs in total.
- An excluded all-generator-sum term (scale $m_P$, restated D4 criterion satisfied) occurs in four graphs: the closed meson line on the five-leg vertex, the $(M,M)$ bubble with $\sigma$ alone at one vertex, and the closed meson line on each $\pi^\pm$ leg. In the other meson-only 1PI graphs this term is exactly zero, because a vertex with three meson legs needs $\widehat P_{0,0}=0$. What remains of them comes from the terms in which at least one meson line takes its ALP-field entry or its mixed entries; the pieces per graph are in the [meson-line piece record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-meson-line-pieces.md).
- At large $\epsilon$, with fixed sources and fixed $\lambda$, the largest power of $X_\Gamma^{\max}/M_0$, $\delta Z^{\max}$ and $R$ is $\epsilon^4$ (graphs 1, 2, 10, 11, 13, σ1, σ2, σ4), in every region of $\kappa_\sigma/A$ ([large-$\epsilon$ graph record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-graphs.md), with the assumptions of the [large-$\epsilon$ input record](murayama-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-inputs.md)). The D4 terms (scale $m_P$) behave relative to $M_0$ as $\epsilon^0$ (graph 3), $\epsilon^2$ (graph 15, through $p_\sigma\cdot p_{\pi^\pm}\sim m_\sigma^2$) and $\epsilon^0$ (π2), below $\epsilon^4$.

## Reproduction

[`derive_sigma_pipi_loop_graphs.py`](../../../scripts/derive_sigma_pipi_loop_graphs.py) with [`sigma_pipi_loop_algebra.py`](../../../scripts/sigma_pipi_loop_algebra.py); tests `test_derive_sigma_pipi_loop_graphs`. The script asserts that there is no box and no $(2,5)$ 1PI graph, and that every graph is kept or vanishes exactly. It identifies the excluded term per graph and checks the restated D4 criterion exactly in every routing, and it computes the termwise routing maximum with the per-term scales.

# Inputs and scope

This record derives the leading WZW contribution to
$a(p_a)\to\gamma(k,\varepsilon_\gamma)P_1(p_1)P_2(p_2)$ and compares a generic
quark-rotation basis with Alda:2026nrd's no-mixing basis. It uses the gauged
$\gamma3P$ vertex in the [WZW record](../../../rcht/odd-parity/wzw-gamma3pi-contribution.md)
and Alda:2026nrd's [source and LSZ conventions](../../../extracts/Alda:2026nrd/appendix-a-expansion.md)
[@Wess:1971yu; @Alda:2026nrd]. The ALP is retained to first order in $1/f_a$,
at tree level and leading anomalous chiral order. Natural units,
$U=\exp(i\Phi/f_\pi)$, $Q=\operatorname{diag}(2/3,-1/3,-1/3)$,
$N_c=3$, and $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$ are used. The common WZW orientation is
denoted by $\mathrm{orientation}$, as in the $\gamma3P$ record. Assume
$[Q,c_q^A]=[Q,S]=0$, where $S\equiv\mathbb M_a^{-1}(C)$ is the invariant
neutral ALP--meson mass-mixing solution. No ALP--meson degeneracy is included.

The executable derivation is
[`scripts/derive_wzw_alp_photon_two_pseudoscalar.py`](../../../scripts/derive_wzw_alp_photon_two_pseudoscalar.py):

```text
.venv/bin/python scripts/derive_wzw_alp_photon_two_pseudoscalar.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/derive_wzw_alp_photon_two_pseudoscalar.py --test
```

# Generic-basis derivation

Let $\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger$ be the outgoing pseudoscalar
generators in Alda's normalization. Define the fully antisymmetrized flavor
trace
$$
\mathfrak F(Q;A,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger)
=\sum_{\sigma\in S_3}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)
\operatorname{tr}\!\left(Q X_{\sigma(1)}X_{\sigma(2)}X_{\sigma(3)}\right),
\qquad (X_1,X_2,X_3)=(A,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger).
$$
The alternating trace is the flavor coefficient of the $\gamma\Phi^3$
exterior product. The LO meson component of the physical ALP is
$$
\frac{\Phi_{\rm mix}}{a}=\frac{2f_\pi}{f_a}(c_q^A+S),
$$
as in the [source-LSZ assembly](../../../rcht/odd-parity/odd-alp-source-lsz-assembly.md).
The direct axial-source insertion in the gauged functional and this meson
insertion have opposite placements in the alternating trace:
$$
\mathfrak F_{\rm dir}=-\mathfrak F(Q;c_q^A,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger),
\qquad
\mathfrak F_{\rm mix}=+\mathfrak F(Q;c_q^A+S,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger).
$$
The sign of the direct term follows from the local axial Ward identity: the
pure-gradient axial source is removed at this order by
$\Phi\mapsto\Phi-2f_\pi c_q^A a/f_a$. The anomalous variation is source-only
and supplies no separate one-photon/two-meson term. Thus the direct term is
the negative of the $\gamma3P$ insertion generated by that field shift; it
cannot be inferred by keeping only the $\gamma3P$ vertex and attaching an
ALP leg.

The Lorentz coefficient from the executable derivation is
$$
\mathcal M_{\rm WZW}=\epsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
\varepsilon_\gamma^\mu p_1^\nu p_2^\rho k^\sigma\,
\mathcal A_{\rm WZW},\qquad
\mathcal A_{\rm WZW}=-\mathrm{orientation}\,
\frac{N_c e}{12\pi^2 f_a f_\pi^2}
\left(\mathfrak F_{\rm dir}+\mathfrak F_{\rm mix}\right).
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_{\rm WZW}=-\mathrm{orientation}\,
\frac{N_c e}{12\pi^2 f_a f_\pi^2}
\mathfrak F(Q;S,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger).}
$$
Under the quark redefinition $c_q^A\to c_q^A+\kappa$, the direct term shifts
by $-\mathfrak F(Q;\kappa,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger)$ and the
mixing term by the opposite amount; their sum is invariant. Here $S$ is
invariant because the mass-mixing source $C$ depends on the invariant
$c_q^{AP}=c_q^A-c_q^P$ and invariant gluonic combination, as in the
source-LSZ record.

# No-mixing basis and channel coefficients

In Alda:2026nrd's no-mixing basis,
$\widehat c_q^A=-S$ and the ALP meson overlap vanishes. Only the direct WZW
vertex remains, with $\mathfrak F_{\rm dir}(\widehat c_q^A)=
\mathfrak F(Q;S,\lambda_1^\dagger,\lambda_2^\dagger)$. It reproduces exactly
the generic-basis sum above.

For the outgoing charged and neutral generators used by the script, the
nonzero flavor coefficients $\mathfrak F(Q;S,\lambda_1^\dagger,
\lambda_2^\dagger)$ are
$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
P_1P_2 & \mathfrak F \\
\hline
\pi^+\pi^- & -2S_u \\
K^+K^- & -2S_u \\
K^0\bar K^0 & 2(S_d-S_s) \\
K^0\pi^0 & \sqrt2 S_{ds} \\
K^0\eta_8 & -\sqrt6 S_{ds} \\
\bar K^0\pi^0 & -\sqrt2 S_{sd} \\
\bar K^0\eta_8 & \sqrt6 S_{sd}
\end{array}
$$
All these coefficients multiply the same Lorentz prefactor in the boxed
formula. Bose-identical pairs vanish. The script also confirms the
$\gamma3\pi$ normalization and the photon Ward identity. The displayed
table is in the octet/singlet generator basis; physical neutral-meson
channels can be obtained by applying the selected meson-mixing rotation to
the external generators.

# Relation to the existing resonance amplitude

This is the local leading WZW term. It must be added coherently to the
$VJP+VVP$ resonance contribution in the
[existing $a\to\gamma PP$ record](alp-photon-pp-amplitude.md). The present
derivation does not include higher-order homogeneous odd operators, loops, or
finite-width/rescattering corrections.

# Verification

The SymPy script constructs the six-term alternating trace without assuming
that flavor matrices commute, derives the common coefficient from the
source-linear $\gamma\Phi^3$ functional and the Alda LSZ overlap, and checks
the generic-basis cancellation, no-mixing identity, invariance of $C$ under
the quark redefinition, charged-channel flavor coefficients, $\gamma3\pi$
normalization, and Ward identity. All seven tests
pass.

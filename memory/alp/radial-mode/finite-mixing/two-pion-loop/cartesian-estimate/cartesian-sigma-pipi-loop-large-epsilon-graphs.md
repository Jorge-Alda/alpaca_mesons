# Cartesian frame: large $\epsilon$ of the graphs, of $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$ and of $R$

Task 4.3 (generic sources) of the OpenSpec change `estimate-sigma-pipi-one-loop-murayama-cartesian`. **No loop integral, scalar loop function, loop coefficient or loop amplitude is computed.** The powers are labels of the exact estimate of the [Cartesian estimate record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-estimate.md); no graph and no term is dropped, and no expansion in $\epsilon$ is made in the estimate itself. Series in $u=1/\epsilon$ are used only to read the leading power and its coefficient.

**Caveat.** The estimate is an order of magnitude per graph class. Graphs with the same $\epsilon$ scaling can cancel in the exact loop, and these cancellations can differ between the Cartesian and the polar parametrization. Agreement with the polar estimate, including its up-only prefactor $-21/(64\pi^2)$, is not expected, and neither estimate is authoritative. The comparison with the polar estimate is in the [polar comparison record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-polar-comparison.md) (task 4.4).

- $X_\Gamma$ and $\delta Z$ of every kept graph in every routing (D3 scales, D4 terms removed): [Cartesian 1PI trace record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-traces-1pi.md), [Cartesian external-leg record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-traces-external-legs.md); graph labels: [Cartesian graph-list record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-list.md).
- Regime D10 in the Cartesian invariants, pole data and their series ($m_a^2=\epsilon^2A+2c_0/A+\dots$, $N(m_a^2)$, $\mathbb M^{-1}_{m_a^2}(\mathcal C)=-\sum_kL^k(\mathcal C)/m_a^{2(k+1)}$, $c_k=\langle\mathcal C L^k(\mathcal C)\rangle$): [Cartesian large-$\epsilon$ input record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-inputs.md).
- Vertex powers and the tree $M_0\sim\epsilon$: [Cartesian large-$\epsilon$ vertex record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-vertices-tree.md).
- Routing rule D12 (R9) and the residue prescription (R10): [Cartesian selection record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-selection.md).

## Regime (assumptions)

1. $\epsilon$ grows at fixed $\widehat S^C_{r,s}$, $\widehat P^C_{r,s}$, $\lambda$, $f_\pi$, $B_0$, $m_1$, $m_P$ and $m_{\pi^\pm}$; $f_a=f_\pi/\epsilon$ varies.
2. $A=B_0(\langle\widehat S^C_{1,0}\rangle-2\langle\widehat S^C_{0,2}\rangle)>0$ and $\kappa_\sigma=B_0(\langle\widehat S^C_{1,0}\rangle-2\langle\widehat S^C_{2,0}\rangle)>0$.
3. $m_\sigma^2=2\lambda f_\pi^2/\epsilon^2+\epsilon^2\kappa_\sigma$ (exact); $m_a^2$ is the root of $D_a$, read as a series.
4. $\epsilon$ is large enough that $m_a,m_\sigma>m_P$, so $\max(m_P,m_a)=m_a$, $\max(m_P,m_\sigma)=m_\sigma$; $\max(m_a,m_\sigma)$ is decided by $r=\kappa_\sigma/A$ (below).
5. Generic sources: distinct products of traces of canonical words are independent; no relation among $3\times3$ matrices is used.

## Method

1. **Exact rewriting before reading powers.** In each $X_\Gamma$ and $\delta Z$: $\mathbb M^{-1}_{m_a^2}(\mathcal C)$ is replaced by its series in the matrices $L^k(\mathcal C)$ (words in $\widehat P^C_{0,1}$, $\widehat S^C_{0,0}$ and $\langle\widehat P^C_{0,1}\rangle$); then $\langle\widehat S^C_{1,0}\rangle=\kappa_\sigma/B_0+2\langle\widehat S^C_{2,0}\rangle$ and $\langle\widehat S^C_{0,2}\rangle=(\kappa_\sigma-A)/(2B_0)+\langle\widehat S^C_{2,0}\rangle$ (exact); then each scalar coefficient is written as a series in $u$ and the $c_k$ are written back as traces. All pieces of a quantity are summed before its leading power is read. Each series carries the first power of $u$ at which it is no longer exact (from the truncated pole root, the truncated $\mathbb M^{-1}$ series and the cut of the inverse of an infinite series); a leading term that would lie beyond it raises an error.
2. **Routing at large $\epsilon$ (D12 restated).** For each product of traces $t$ of a graph, the routing is chosen by the largest leading power of $\epsilon$ of $C_t^\rho$, then by the largest magnitude of its leading coefficient (with $\kappa_\sigma=rA$), then by the next coefficients. Exact ties follow D12. Only $C12$, $C13$, $C15$ depend on the routing (18, 34 and 34 products).
3. **Regions.** The open intervals of $r$ between $r=1$ (where $\max(m_a,m_\sigma)$ and the Piecewise conditions of the trace records change) and the positive roots $r=1/2,\,3/2,\,5/2$ of $|{\rm lead}_\rho(r)|=|{\rm lead}_{\rho'}(r)|$ are each checked at three points ($1/8,1/4,3/8$; $5/8,3/4,7/8$; $9/8,5/4,11/8$; $7/4,2,9/4$; $3,7/2,10$), and the boundary points separately. Neighbouring intervals with the same choices merge into two regions: $r<1$ (routing 3 for every routing-dependent product) and $r>1$ (routing 4); at $r=1/2$ and $r=1$ the choice is routing 3, at $r=3/2$ and $5/2$ routing 4. No tie occurs and no product is marked. At every sample and boundary point the large-$\epsilon$ choice equals the branch of the termwise maximum of the trace records with its mass conditions evaluated at large $\epsilon$, where $m_a^2-m_\sigma^2\to\epsilon^2(A-\kappa_\sigma)$ for $r\neq1$ and $\to2c_0/A=2B_0^2\langle\widehat P^C_{0,1}\widehat P^C_{0,1}\rangle/A>0$ at $r=1$, so $\max(m_a,m_\sigma)=m_a$ for $r\le1$ and $m_\sigma$ for $r>1$.
4. **Shares of $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$.** $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0=\sum_\Gamma X_\Gamma/M_0+\frac12(\delta Z_s+\delta Z_{\pi^+}+\delta Z_{\pi^-})$, with $M_0=-\frac{B_0\epsilon}{2f_\pi}\langle\widehat S^C_{1,0}\{\lambda_{\pi^+},\lambda_{\pi^-}\}\rangle$. Each share is written as $\epsilon^p$ times a sum of products of traces, divided by $\langle\widehat S^C_{1,0}\{\lambda_{\pi^+},\lambda_{\pi^-}\}\rangle$; this sum is called its leading numerator below.
5. **Groups with the same leading power.** For each group the script states "same sign" if all leading numerators are proportional to one trace polynomial with positive ratios at the sample points, "could cancel" if they are proportional with a negative ratio somewhere, and "depends on sources" otherwise; "single graph" for a group of one. It also lists the pairs of graphs that share a product of traces with coefficients of opposite sign, and the pairs whose leading terms add up to zero exactly. A statement "could cancel" or "depends on sources" does not assert that the graphs cancel in the exact loop.

## Summary of the generic result

- In every region, $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0\sim\epsilon^4$ and $R=|M_1^{\rm est}|/|M_0|\sim\epsilon^4$, with the leading coefficients written out above. The graphs that reach $\epsilon^4$ are $C1$, $C2$, $C10$, $C11$, $C12$ (1PI) and $C\sigma4$, $C\sigma5$, $C\sigma6$ ($s$ leg). The leading numerator has no positive root in $r$ at which all its coefficients vanish.
- Between the regions only coefficients change, through $\max(m_a,m_\sigma)$ and the routing choice: at $\epsilon^4$, $C\sigma5$ has $1/A$ for $r<1$ and at $r=1$, and $1/\kappa_\sigma$ for $r>1$; at $r=1$, $\kappa_\sigma$ is replaced by $A$ in $C1$ and $C\sigma4$. The powers of all graphs are the same in the three cases.
- The $\epsilon^4$ group is "depends on sources": its eight numerators are not proportional to one trace polynomial, so whether they add or cancel depends on the sources. In the $\epsilon^2$ group, $C13$, $C15$ and $C3$ share products of traces with coefficients of opposite sign; in the $\epsilon^0$ group the leading terms of $C\pi2$ and $C\pi3$ add up to zero exactly, as do their full $\delta Z$ ([external-leg record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-traces-external-legs.md)).
- The terms that set the leading power come from source vertices with $r+s=n$ legs ($\widehat S^C_{3,0}$, $\widehat S^C_{1,2}$, $\widehat S^C_{2,1}$ on the $s$ side, $\widehat S^C_{2,0}$, $\widehat S^C_{1,1}$, $\widehat S^C_{0,2}$ at $\{\pi^+,\pi^-\}$), each carrying $\epsilon^{r+s}$, combined with $m_\sigma^2\sim\epsilon^2\kappa_\sigma$ or $m_a^2\sim\epsilon^2A$ from the size rule ($C1$, $C2$) or the residue derivative ($C\sigma4$–$C\sigma6$). The $V_\varphi$ vertices, of order $\epsilon^{-1}$ at fixed $\lambda$, do not reach $\epsilon^4$.

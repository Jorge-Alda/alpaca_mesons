# Cartesian estimate

Independent size estimate of the one-loop $\sigma\to\pi^+\pi^-$ amplitude in the Cartesian fields $\varphi=f_a+s+ip$, written in the invariants $\widehat S^C_{r,s}$, $\widehat P^C_{r,s}$ (OpenSpec change `estimate-sigma-pipi-one-loop-murayama-cartesian`). The estimate is an order of magnitude per graph class; neither it nor the polar estimate is authoritative.

- [Frame and tree](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-frame-and-tree.md) — Cartesian Lagrangian, assumptions restated in $\widehat S^C$, $\widehat P^C$ and derived from the linear and quadratic Lagrangian, pole data, CP, charge rule and tree $M_0$ with its up-only value.
- [Vertex rules](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-vertex-rules.md) — Cartesian vertices with three to five legs (sources, $V_\varphi$, four-meson kinetic term), checked by direct expansion, relabelling, dimensions and the up-only limit.
- [Tree equivalence](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-tree-equivalence.md) — The only polar comparison: on-shell $V(s;\pi^+,\pi^-)$ equals the polar tree through the first-order relation between the Cartesian and polar coefficients.
- [Large-epsilon inputs](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-inputs.md) — Regime D10 in the Cartesian invariants, leading powers of the pole data, of the meson-line residue pieces and of the leg factors, generic and up-only.
- [Large-epsilon vertices and tree](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-vertices-tree.md) — Powers of $\epsilon$ of the Cartesian vertices by field-end pattern with $\lambda$ fixed, generic and up-only, and of the tree.
- [Graph selection](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-selection.md) — Rules D1–D5, D11, D12, the D4 criterion, the tadpole assumption and the residue prescription restated in the Cartesian invariants, each linked to its polar rule.
- [Graph list](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-list.md) — One-loop graphs and external self-energies enumerated from the Cartesian vertices, with $S_\Gamma$, $r_\Gamma$, loop scales, meson-line pieces, counts per class, excluded D4 terms and the role of the $V_\varphi$ vertices.
- [1PI traces](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-traces-1pi.md) — Compact traces of the kept 1PI graphs with per-term loop scales, termwise routing maximum, its mass-dependent branches and the routing spread.
- [External-leg traces](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-traces-external-legs.md) — Residue corrections $\delta Z$ of the $s$ and $\pi^\pm$ legs in compact traces, with the residue prescription and the cancellation of the closed ALP-like and meson lines on the pion leg.
- [Estimate](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-estimate.md) — Assembly of $M_1^{\rm est}$ and $R$ from the kept graphs and residues, with dimension checks, the vanishing-tree case, the source letters and the cancellation of $\widehat S^C_{0,0}$.
- [Frame invariance](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-frame-invariance.md) — The traces of the estimate are unchanged under a constant axial redefinition of the sources, with a negative control on the raw coefficients.
- [Large-epsilon graphs](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-graphs.md) — Leading powers and coefficients of every kept graph, of $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$ and of $R$ at large $\epsilon$ for generic sources, regions of $\kappa_\sigma/A$, D4 powers and groups of graphs with the same power.
- [Up-only estimate](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-up-only-estimate.md) — Leading large-$\epsilon$ term of $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$ for up-only sources, set by the all-generator term of $C\sigma9$, and its agreement from both neighbouring regions.
- [Limits](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-limits.md) — Limits and assumptions of the Cartesian estimate, the order-of-magnitude caveat and the size of the difference between the Cartesian and polar vacuum conditions.
- [Polar comparison](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-polar-comparison.md) — Diagnostic comparison with the polar estimate per graph class, region and for up-only sources, with the order-of-magnitude caveat and the statements on possible cancellations.
- [Up-only $m_P$ and unitarity](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-up-only-mp-and-unitarity.md) — Identification of the loop scale $m_P$ with the $\eta'$-like pole in the up-only model, the $\epsilon$ at which $R=1$, and the reading of tree-level unitarity as the bound on $\epsilon$ and of $R(\epsilon)$ as an estimate of the uncertainty.

# Diagnostic comparison of the Cartesian and the polar size estimates of $\sigma\to\pi^+\pi^-$ at one loop

Task 4.4 of the OpenSpec change `estimate-sigma-pipi-one-loop-murayama-cartesian`. Script: `scripts/derive_sigma_pipi_cartesian_loop_polar_comparison.py` (run with `--markdown`; the last section is its output, tested in `scripts/test_derive_sigma_pipi_cartesian_loop_polar_comparison.py`). The script reads the results of the Cartesian scripts and of the existing polar scripts. No loop integral, scalar loop function, loop coefficient or loop amplitude is evaluated, and no numerical matrix is used. The comparison is made after both derivations were complete and enters neither of them (the only polar input of the Cartesian derivation is the tree equivalence, [tree-equivalence record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-tree-equivalence.md)).

## Caveat

Both results are orders of magnitude per graph class. Graphs with the same $\epsilon$ scaling can have leading coefficients that cancel in the exact loop, and such cancellations can be missed in one parametrization and not in the other, because the size rule acts on different vertices and lines. Agreement between the two estimates, including with the polar up-only prefactor $-21/(64\pi^2)$, is not expected. Neither estimate is authoritative. Only the exact one-loop total, with tadpoles and residues, is independent of the parametrization. Differences below are reported, not attributed to either parametrization being correct.

## Results

The script output, reproduced verbatim in the last section, has the following parts, in this order: the map between the coefficients (Taylor expansion of $1+(s+ip)/f_a=(1+\sigma/f_a)e^{ia/f_a}$ through third order, derivation in the [tree-equivalence record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-tree-equivalence.md)) with the checks of $A$, $\kappa_\sigma$, $C$, $L$, $m_\sigma^2$ and $m_a^2$; the boundaries of the regions of $r=\kappa_\sigma/A$ in each parametrization; the D4 excluded terms, equal routing by routing after the map; the table of graph classes; the powers per topology and region; the total $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$ per region; the up-only leading terms and the table of up-only shares per class.

A class is a topology with the types of its internal lines; a meson line is typed by the pieces that contribute to it, (i) the generator-sum piece, (ii)-(iv) the ALP-sector pieces ([Cartesian graph list](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-graph-list.md)). Observations:

1. The classes and the number of graphs per class coincide in the two parametrizations, and no class occurs in only one of them. For generic sources the leading power of every class share ($r<1$) and of every topology is the same in the two parametrizations and in all regions.
2. The total $M_1^{\rm est}/M_0$ has the leading power $\epsilon^4$ in both parametrizations and in every region, but the leading coefficients are different functions of the invariants: the Cartesian numerator written in the polar coefficients differs from the polar numerator (column "numerators equal after the map" is "no"; the difference for $r<1$ is in the script output). The coefficients themselves are in the [Cartesian large-epsilon record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-graphs.md) and the polar records and are not copied here.
3. For up-only sources the leading power differs: $\epsilon^2$ in the Cartesian estimate (from $C\sigma9$) and $\epsilon^4$ in the polar one (from graphs 2, 13, $\sigma4$), and the polar prefactor $-21/(64\pi^2)$ is not reproduced. The classes $C30$, $C40$, $C\sigma4$, $C\sigma9$, $C\pi2$, $C\pi3$ and $C\pi8$ have equal up-only leading terms in the two parametrizations; the other classes differ in power or coefficient. The Cartesian counterparts $C2$, $C12$, $C\sigma6$ of the polar graphs that lead at $\epsilon^4$ are at $\epsilon^0$, $\epsilon^{-2}$, $\epsilon^{-4}$. Whether this reflects a cancellation in the exact loop, or the different vertices, is not decided by the estimate.

## Possible cancellations among graphs with the same power

In each parametrization the script applies the same test to the graphs of a class with the largest individual power: "same sign" (leading numerators proportional with positive ratios at the sample points), "could cancel" (proportional with a negative ratio somewhere), "depends on sources" (not proportional to one trace polynomial), "single graph". The class table lists the outcome for both. None of these statements asserts that graphs cancel in the exact loop. For the Cartesian groups at each leading power see the [Cartesian large-epsilon record](cartesian-sigma-pipi-loop-large-epsilon-graphs.md); in particular $C\pi2$ and $C\pi3$ cancel exactly in $\delta Z_{\pi^\pm}$ at leading order, as do their polar counterparts for up-only sources (the equal up-only values above with opposite sign).

## Reading of the diagnostic

1. Classes, graph counts and leading powers per class agree between the parametrizations for generic sources.
2. Leading coefficients differ, and the leading power for up-only sources differs. This is expected given that each class is an estimate and that the Cartesian and polar vertices are different functions of the sources.
3. No conclusion is drawn on which estimate is closer to the exact result. A reliable statement on the size of the one-loop amplitude needs the full loop, outside the scope of this change.

# Inputs, conventions, and scope

This derives the retained sequential $\rho$-exchange term in the amputated
transverse vertex $V_i^*(q)\to\pi^+(k_1)\pi^-(k_2)\pi^0(k_3)$, with
$q=k_1+k_2+k_3$ and $s=q^2$. The calculation uses the original VVP
$d_1,\ldots,d_4$ operators and the leading even-parity $G_V$ VPP vertex in
the antisymmetric-tensor representation. Their definitions and
normalization are those in the [RChT
records](../../rcht/foundations/rcht-basics.md) and the source-backed
[three-pion VVP extract](../../extracts/Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx/vvp-three-pseudoscalar.md)
[@Alda:2026nrd; @Ecker:1988te; @Ecker:1989yg; @Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx].

Use natural units, metric $(+---)$, $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$, equal pion masses,
and isospin-symmetric diagonal vector generators. Define
$s_{12}=(k_1+k_2)^2$, $s_{13}=(k_1+k_3)^2$, and
$s_{23}=(k_2+k_3)^2$. The vector source is projected as
$$
V_i^{\mu\nu}
=\frac{q^\mu\varepsilon_i^\nu-q^\nu\varepsilon_i^\mu}{M_i},
\qquad q\cdot\varepsilon_i=0,
$$
also when $s\ne M_i^2$. The fixed $M_i$ normalization defines this
off-shell convention; it is not an LSZ state away from the pole. Replacing
$M_i$ by $\sqrt{s}$ in the source projection rescales the displayed scalar
by $M_i/\sqrt{s}$. On-shell data do not choose between these continuations.

The result is only the sequential exchange generated by the retained
operators. It is not the complete off-shell amplitude. The $VVP$ basis is
complete for one-pseudoscalar vertices, while a direct $V3\pi$ term is also
leading. A five-operator local odd-parity $VPPP$ basis is given in
[@GomezDumm:2010ra]; the present retained $d_i,G_V$ calculation does not
include or match its coefficients. The
[contact-operator reduction](../../rcht/odd-parity/vector-three-pion-contact.md)
shows that the tested derivative contact is a combination of existing
$VPPP$ basis terms modulo the stated EOM and a double trace. Pion
rescattering and the corresponding complex Khuri--Treiman solution are
separate inputs [@Niecknig:2012sj].

# Lorentz contractions and exchange numerator

For a chosen pair with invariant $s_{ij}$ and spectator pion, contract both
placements of the external and internal vector tensors in each VVP operator
with the epsilon tensor. After dividing by the common external tensor
$$
\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
k_1^\alpha k_2^\beta k_3^\rho\varepsilon_i^\sigma,
$$
the executed SymPy contractions give
$$
\mathcal O^1:\frac{2m_\pi^2}{M_i},\qquad
\mathcal O^2:\frac{8}{M_i},\qquad
\mathcal O^3:-\frac{2(s+s_{ij}-m_\pi^2)}{M_i},\qquad
\mathcal O^4:0.
$$
Restoring the Fourier and $\chi_-$ factors, then multiplying by the leading
$G_V$ VPP vertex, gives the numerator shared by each of the three pair
channels:
$$
m_\pi^2(d_1+8d_2-d_3)+d_3(s+s_{ij}).
$$
The internal vector denominator is the retained octet mass $M_V$, as in the
source expression; it is not identified here with the physical $\rho$
mass. The sum over the three pairings is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M^{\mathrm{seq}}_{i,\mathrm{fixed}\ M_i}
={}&i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
k_1^\alpha k_2^\beta k_3^\rho\varepsilon_i^\sigma\,
\sqrt{2}(T_i)_{uu}\frac{8G_V}{M_iF^3}\\
&\times\sum_{(ij)=(12),(13),(23)}
\frac{m_\pi^2(d_1+8d_2-d_3)+d_3(s+s_{ij})}
{M_V^2-s_{ij}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $(T_i)_{uu}=(T_i)_{dd}$ is the nonstrange diagonal entry of the
isoscalar generator $T_i=\operatorname{diag}((T_i)_{uu},(T_i)_{uu},
(T_i)_{ss})$. For ideal $\omega$, $(T_\omega)_{uu}=1/\sqrt2$, so the flavor
factor is one. For ideal $\phi$, $(T_\phi)_{uu}=0$, so this retained
single-trace term vanishes. A nonzero $\phi\to3\pi$ amplitude then requires
nonideal mixing, additional flavor structures, or other contributions.

# On-shell comparison and interpretation

Set $s=M_\omega^2$ and $(T_\omega)_{uu}=1/\sqrt2$. The expression reduces
exactly to the source-backed $\rho$-exchange term
$$
\frac{8G_V}{M_\omega F^3}
\sum_{(ij)=(12),(13),(23)}
\frac{m_\pi^2(d_1+8d_2-d_3)+d_3(M_\omega^2+s_{ij})}
{M_V^2-s_{ij}},
$$
which is the on-shell $\omega\to3\pi$ formula in the
[Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx extract](../../extracts/Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx/omega-three-pion-amplitude.md).
This check fixes the retained on-shell normalization; it does not make the
off-shell continuation representation independent.

The retained expression has zero width in the internal pair propagators.
The dispersive treatment of $V_i\to3\pi$ instead places the $\pi\pi$
P-wave rescattering in its Omnès solution and includes crossed-channel
effects [@Niecknig:2012sj]. The tree expression can serve as a model
contribution or low-order driving term, but it is not to be added to that
solution without a matching prescription. Likewise, the off-shell external
vector vertex is field dependent: regular terms and direct $V3\pi$
operators can shift under field redefinitions while leaving the on-shell
amplitude unchanged.

# Local contact completion and remaining inputs

The source-backed five-operator local endpoint and its contraction with the
full antisymmetric-tensor ALP production source are now derived in the
[contact record](alp-isoscalar-vector-three-pion-contact.md). Its independent
$g_i$ are not fixed by the retained VVP calculation. The contact endpoint
also has a complementary tensor component, so the strict spin-one pole
residue alone does not contain its full tree contribution.

- A physical finite-width prediction needs a rescattered complex
  three-pion amplitude, compatible subtraction and normalization inputs,
  and a common phase with the ALP production residue.
- The off-shell extension of the decay amplitude is not an observable by
  itself. Its normalization must match the chosen vector interpolating
  field and self-energy; the complete current or ALP production amplitude
  must retain the accompanying regular terms.
- In the ideal-mixing single-trace truncation the $\phi$ term is zero. The
  physical $\phi\to3\pi$ amplitude requires additional flavor/mixing input.

# Reproducibility

The executable
[derive_offshell_vector_three_pion.py](../../../scripts/derive_offshell_vector_three_pion.py)
uses SymPy to contract both vector placements for the four VVP operators,
derive the common numerator and three crossed $\rho$ pairings, and evaluate
the flavor traces. Run:

    .venv/bin/python scripts/derive_offshell_vector_three_pion.py

The execution prints all intermediate contractions and the final amplitude.
Four focused tests pass: the on-shell $\omega$ source formula, transversality,
the propagating tensor projector, and ideal flavor limits. They validate
this fixed-convention tree contribution, not the missing contact terms or
the physical off-shell amplitude.

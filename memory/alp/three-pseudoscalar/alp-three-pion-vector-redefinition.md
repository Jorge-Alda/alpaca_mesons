# Finite-mixing three-pion amplitude under a vector redefinition

## Inputs and assumptions

Use the leading chiral Lagrangian and cubic and quartic vertices from the [finite-mixing three-pion derivation](alp-finite-mixing-three-pion-amplitudes.md), together with the mixed-pole conventions in the [finite-mixing record](../shared/finite-alp-meson-mixing.md), following `Alda:2026nrd` [@Alda:2026nrd]. Work at tree level in natural units, with the numerical inputs $f_\pi=f_a=B_0=1$ in a common unit of mass; in particular, this choice assumes $f_\pi/f_a=1$. The quark masses are diagonal and equal; the only nonzero ALP source is a pseudoscalar coupling proportional to $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$. Set the anomaly contribution to zero. The vacuum is stationary and parity even at $a=0$. These are assumptions for this example, not approximations to general couplings.

## Derivation

1. Choose the two physical neutral squared masses $36$ and $1$ and normalized pole columns in the $(a,\pi^0)$ basis $(12/13,5/13)$ and $(-5/13,12/13)$. The bare neutral-pion squared mass is their exact projection $1044/169$, which also fixes the charged-pion squared mass in this isospin-symmetric example. The off-diagonal mass entry fixes the pseudoscalar source to $-175/174$. The script constructs the quadratic matrix and verifies both eigenvectors and eigenvalues; no expansion in the mixing is used. The decay is above threshold.

2. At the on-shell point $s=124/5$ and $t=u=20749/1690$, evaluate the full leading-order quartic mass and kinetic vertices in the two physical neutral pole directions and the charged-pion directions. The existing vertex expressions give
$$
\mathcal M(a_{\rm phys}\to\pi^0_{\rm phys}\pi^+\pi^-)=\frac{188832}{28561}.
$$
All original cubic vertices vanish for these source choices. Thus there are no exchange diagrams in the original coordinates at this order. The incoming momentum and outgoing momenta are placed in the all-incoming convention of the linked derivation.

3. Make the charge-conserving vector redefinition generated by $\lambda_3/2$, with phase proportional to the original ALP field. Choose the group matrices $g_R=g_L=\exp[-i a\lambda_3/(2f_a)]$, so that the charged pion fields acquire opposite phases $e^{\pm ia/f_a}$. The original ALP coordinate is the linear combination $(12a_{\rm phys}-5\pi^0_{\rm phys})/13$. Expanding the transformed charged kinetic term through fourth order produces cubic vertices with either neutral pole and a quartic vertex with both. Differentiating the transformed kinetic term and using the on-shell invariants gives the following contributions in the same amplitude convention:
$$
\mathcal M_{\rm contact}=-\frac{732}{169},\qquad
\mathcal M_t=\mathcal M_u=\frac{366}{169}.
$$
The $s$-channel exchange vanishes for these source choices. Therefore the quartic contribution and both charged-pion exchange diagrams cancel, and the transformed amplitude equals the result in step 2. Dropping either the induced contact or the exchanges leaves a nonzero discrepancy. The remaining leading-order quartic interactions can be evaluated at the linear part of this map because their nonlinear corrections begin at fifth field order.

## Verification and limitations

The script retains the rotation coefficient symbolically and evaluates the exact mass and kinetic vertices from the linked derivation, obtains the new cubic and quartic terms by differentiation, checks the pole equations and physical threshold, and verifies the cancellation. A unit test checks the nonzero individual terms and the final equality. This is one exact-rational, charge-conserving example, not a numerical survey or a proof for arbitrary source matrices.

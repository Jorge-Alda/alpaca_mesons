# Axial-vector resonances in the $\langle VAP\rangle$ Green function (Cirigliano:2004ue)

Single-multiplet (one $V$, one $A$, one $P$ nonet), leading order in $1/N_C$. Source: Cirigliano:2004ue, Sec. on the chiral resonance Lagrangian and short-distance constraints.

**Field content and kinetic terms.** $V_{\mu\nu}$, $A_{\mu\nu}$ are antisymmetric tensor nonets ($1^{--}$, $1^{++}$), $P$ a pseudoscalar nonet (Cirigliano:2004ue):
$$\mathcal{L}^R_{\rm kin}=-\tfrac12\langle\nabla^\lambda R_{\lambda\mu}\nabla_\nu R^{\nu\mu}-\tfrac{M_R^2}{2}R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\rangle,\quad R=V,A.$$

**Linear couplings** (Cirigliano:2004ue, Eq. "lag1"; coefficients $F_V,G_V,F_A,d_m$ real):
$$\mathcal{L}_2^{V,A,P}=\tfrac{F_V}{2\sqrt2}\langle V_{\mu\nu}f_+^{\mu\nu}\rangle+i\tfrac{G_V}{\sqrt2}\langle V_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu\rangle+\tfrac{F_A}{2\sqrt2}\langle A_{\mu\nu}f_-^{\mu\nu}\rangle+i d_m\langle P\chi_-\rangle.$$

**Bilinear couplings (resonance pairs).** With $h_{\mu\nu}$ and $\nabla$ as in Ecker:1989yg (Cirigliano:2004ue, Eqs. OVA, OPV, OPA):
- $\mathcal{L}_2^{VA}=\sum_{i=1}^5\lambda_i^{VA}\mathcal{O}_i^{VA}$ with $\mathcal{O}_1=\langle[V^{\mu\nu},A_{\mu\nu}]\chi_-\rangle$, $\mathcal{O}_2=i\langle[V^{\mu\nu},A_{\nu\alpha}]h_\mu{}^\alpha\rangle$, $\mathcal{O}_3=i\langle[\nabla^\mu V_{\mu\nu},A^{\nu\alpha}]u_\alpha\rangle$, $\mathcal{O}_4=i\langle[\nabla_\alpha V_{\mu\nu},A^{\alpha\nu}]u^\mu\rangle$, $\mathcal{O}_5=i\langle[\nabla_\alpha V_{\mu\nu},A^{\mu\nu}]u^\alpha\rangle$ (first introduced in GomezDumm:2003ku).
- $\mathcal{L}_2^{PV}$: $\mathcal{O}_1^{PV}=i\langle[\nabla^\mu P,V_{\mu\nu}]u^\nu\rangle$, $\mathcal{O}_2^{PV}=i\langle[P,V_{\mu\nu}]f_-^{\mu\nu}\rangle$.
- $\mathcal{L}_2^{PA}$: $\mathcal{O}_1^{PA}=i\langle[P,A_{\mu\nu}]f_+^{\mu\nu}\rangle$ (the only $PA$ operator entering $\langle VAP\rangle$).

**Combinations** (Cirigliano:2004ue, Eq. LambdaDef):
$$\sqrt2\lambda_0=-4\lambda_1^{VA}-\lambda_2^{VA}-\tfrac{\lambda_4^{VA}}2-\lambda_5^{VA},\quad
\sqrt2\lambda'=\lambda_2^{VA}-\lambda_3^{VA}+\tfrac{\lambda_4^{VA}}2+\lambda_5^{VA},\quad
\sqrt2\lambda''=\lambda_2^{VA}-\tfrac{\lambda_4^{VA}}2-\lambda_5^{VA}.$$

**Single-multiplet relations** (Cirigliano:2004ue, Eq. FVGVFA, citing Ecker:1989yg): $F_VG_V=F^2$, $F_V^2-F_A^2=F^2$, $F_V^2M_V^2=F_A^2M_A^2$; and $d_m=F/(2\sqrt2)$ (cited from the scalar/pseudoscalar analysis, not re-derived in the paper).

**Results** (Cirigliano:2004ue, Eq. lambda):
$$\lambda'=\frac{M_A}{2\sqrt2M_V},\quad\lambda''=\frac{M_A^2-2M_V^2}{2\sqrt2M_VM_A},\quad4\lambda_0=\lambda'+\lambda'',$$
$$\lambda_1^{PV}=-4\lambda_2^{PV},\quad\lambda_2^{PV}=\frac{\sqrt{M_A^2-M_V^2}}{8M_A},\quad\lambda_1^{PA}=\frac{\sqrt{M_A^2-M_V^2}}{8M_V}.$$
Also $c_1=\tfrac12(M_V^2+M_A^2)$ (a mass parameter in the general Green-function ansatz).

**Axial form factor and $a_1\to\pi\gamma$.** Single-resonance $G_A(t)=\frac{2F_VG_V-F_V^2}{M_V^2}+\frac{F_A^2}{M_A^2-t}$ (Ecker:1989yg); with bilinear couplings the KSFR relation is corrected, $1-F_V^2/(2F^2)=\frac{M_A^2-2M_V^2}{2(M_A^2-M_V^2)}$, and
$$\Gamma(a_1\to\pi\gamma)=\frac{\alpha M_A}{24}\Big(\frac{M_A^2}{M_V^2}-1\Big)^3\Big(1-\frac{M_\pi^2}{M_A^2}\Big)^3$$
(Cirigliano:2004ue). Quoted numbers: $1.33$ MeV for $M_A=1.23$ GeV, $M_V=0.771$ GeV.

**Proca vs antisymmetric tensor.** The paper notes (Intro) that an earlier claim (Knecht:2001xc, not in the bibliography here) that a minimal Proca Lagrangian cannot reproduce the asymptotics of $\langle VAP\rangle$ should be contrasted with the antisymmetric-tensor result; the paper itself uses the antisymmetric tensor (Cirigliano:2004ue). Statement about Knecht:2001xc is the paper's, unverified against that source.

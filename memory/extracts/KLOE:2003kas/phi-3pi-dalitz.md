The arXiv source archive is cached at `memory/sources/KLOE:2003kas/arxiv-source.tar`. KLOE fits the efficiency-corrected $\phi\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ Dalitz density, with the density expressed through the charged-$\rho$ amplitudes and their fitted mass/width hypotheses [@KLOE:2003kas]. This shape constrains the three-pseudoscalar $VVP$ amplitude and $\rho$ propagator treatment; the total partial width supplies its normalization. The paper describes statistical and Monte-Carlo efficiency uncertainties but no reusable full covariance matrix is present. The user-authorized diagonal approximation may be applied to quoted fit errors only after an appropriate predictor is supplied; it is not a reconstructed bin-level likelihood.

KLOE does not publish reusable bin contents in the cached source; its tabulated fit-a parameters are preserved in [`data/odd_parity/kloe_phi_3pi_dalitz_fit.csv`](../../../data/odd_parity/kloe_phi_3pi_dalitz_fit.csv). Blank $\rho^0$ and $\rho^-$ mass/width cells are preserved as printed: fit(a) prints the shared constrained values only on the $\rho^+$ rows. CMD-2 tabulated Dalitz inputs are unavailable in the cached Tier-0 material and are not reconstructed.

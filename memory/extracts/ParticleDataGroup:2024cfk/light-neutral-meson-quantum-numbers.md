# Light neutral meson quantum numbers

The 2024 PDG particle listings give the following assignments and fitted masses. The quantum numbers below are transcribed from the listing headers; the displayed $J^{PC}$ values directly provide the quoted $C$ and $P$ assignments.

| particle | PDG listing header | fitted mass |
|---|---|---|
| $\pi^0$ | $I^G(J^{PC})=1^-(0^{-+})$ | $134.9768\pm0.0005\,\mathrm{MeV}$ |
| $\eta$ | $I^G(J^{PC})=0^+(0^{-+})$ | $547.862\pm0.017\,\mathrm{MeV}$ |
| $\eta'(958)$ | $I^G(J^{PC})=0^+(0^{-+})$ | $957.78\pm0.06\,\mathrm{MeV}$ |
| $\omega(782)$ | $I^G(J^{PC})=0^-(1^{--})$ | $782.66\pm0.13\,\mathrm{MeV}$ |
| $\phi(1020)$ | $I^G(J^{PC})=0^-(1^{--})$ | $1019.461\pm0.016\,\mathrm{MeV}$ |

Sources: the $\pi^0$, $\eta$, $\eta'(958)$, $\omega(782)$, and $\phi(1020)$ listings, respectively, page 1 of `rpp2024-list-pi-zero.pdf`, `rpp2024-list-eta.pdf`, `rpp2024-list-eta-prime-958.pdf`, `rpp2024-list-omega-782.pdf`, and `rpp2024-list-phi-1020.pdf` in the cached source directory. [@ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk]

Thus the PDG listings support $C=+$ for the three neutral pseudoscalars and $C=-$ for $\omega$ and $\phi$; the corresponding parity assignments are $P=-$ for all five states. These are literature statements, not derived from the RChT conventions.

The PDG photon listing explicitly gives $I(J^{PC})=0,1(1^{--})$ in its header, so it supports the standard assignment $C(\gamma)=-$ and $P(\gamma)=-$ in the listing convention. It does not quote a measured nonzero photon mass: the listing reports mass upper limits, including $m_\gamma<1\times10^{-18}\,\mathrm{eV}$ from RYUTOV 07, and notes that no finite mass is established. [@ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk, `rpp2024-list-photon.pdf`, p. 1]

# PDG 2025 $\eta'$ mass

The 2025 update to the Review of Particle Physics lists the fitted mass of $\eta'(958)$ as $957.78\pm0.06\,\mathrm{MeV}$ (page 1, "MASS" listing) [@ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk]. The listing itself cites S. Navas et al. (Particle Data Group), *Phys. Rev. D* 110, 030001 (2024) and 2025 update; this is the existing bibliography entry `ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk`.

The official listing PDF is [`rpp2025-list-eta-prime-958.pdf`](../../sources/ParticleDataGroup:2024cfk/rpp2025-list-eta-prime-958.pdf), SHA-256 `3071dd2ae9f3a602f34523b296ba769c23b87026885a4f1bb01b5bab910909e6`. The implied squared-mass uncertainty, if needed, is obtained by ordinary first-order propagation; no squared-mass average is quoted by the listing.

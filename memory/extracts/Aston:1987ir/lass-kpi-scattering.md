LASS extracts $K^-\pi^+$ partial-wave amplitudes from $K^-p\to K^-\pi^+n$ at an $11\,\mathrm{GeV}/c$ beam momentum. The physical charge amplitude requires an $I=3/2$ subtraction to isolate the $I=1/2$ wave. [Aston:1987ir]

Elastic unitarity is imposed on the $S$ wave below $1.29\,\mathrm{GeV}$ rather than independently measured there. The high-energy analysis has solution ambiguities above approximately $1.84\,\mathrm{GeV}$. [Aston:1987ir]

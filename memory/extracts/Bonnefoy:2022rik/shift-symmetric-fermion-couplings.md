# Shift-symmetric ALP fermion couplings and basis changes

`Bonnefoy:2022rik` compares a dimension-five basis with generic ALP-dependent
fermion Yukawa matrices to a basis with derivative couplings to fermion
currents. Its dimension-five quark-sector discussion treats the flavor
matrices as general and determines when those couplings can be represented
by an exactly shift-symmetric derivative basis [@Bonnefoy:2022rik,
Secs. 2--3, Eqs. (2.1)--(2.3)].

The paper also notes that derivative couplings to conserved fermion currents
can be removed by an ALP-dependent flavor transformation at this order, and
that axial transformations shift gauge couplings through mixed anomalies
[@Bonnefoy:2022rik, Sec. 2, Eq. (2.1) and following discussion].

This is a dimension-five electroweak EFT result. It is useful as a precedent
for distinguishing shift-symmetric derivative couplings from spurion
couplings and for tracking field-redefinition freedom. It does not enumerate
the full higher-dimensional QCD bilinear basis requested in the accompanying
derived record; extrapolating its dimension-five coefficient count to that
basis would be unsupported.

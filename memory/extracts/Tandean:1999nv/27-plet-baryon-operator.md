# Leading 27-plet hyperon operator

Tandean:1999nv, Sec. II, Eq. (1), states that the $|\Delta S|=1$, $|\Delta I|=3/2$ Hamiltonian transforms as $(27_L,1_R)$ and gives the leading heavy-baryon chiral Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal L^w=\beta_{27} T_{ij,kl}
(\xi\bar B_v\xi^\dagger)_{ki}(\xi B_v\xi^\dagger)_{lj}
+\delta_{27}(\text{decuplet bilinear dressed with }\xi)
+\mathrm{h.c.}.
$$

Here $\beta_{27}$ couples two octet baryons and $\delta_{27}$ couples two decuplet baryons. This source gives the operator structure and cites its derivation; see AbdEl-Hady:1998qww for the four-quark operator, the explicit tensor entries, and the convention definitions.

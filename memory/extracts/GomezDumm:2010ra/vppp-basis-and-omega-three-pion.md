# Five-operator basis

In the antisymmetric-tensor representation, the source writes the local
odd-parity interaction as
$$
\mathcal L_4^V=\sum_{i=1}^5\frac{g_i}{M_V}\mathcal O^i_{VPPP}
+\sum_{i=1}^7\frac{c_i}{M_V}\mathcal O^i_{VJP},
$$
with real dimensionless couplings; see Eq. (12) of
[@GomezDumm:2010ra]. Its five $VPPP$ operators (Eq. (13)) are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O^1_{VPPP}={}&i\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\left\langle
V^{\mu\nu}\left(h^{\alpha\gamma}u_\gamma u^\beta
-u^\beta u_\gamma h^{\alpha\gamma}\right)\right\rangle,\\
\mathcal O^2_{VPPP}={}&i\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\left\langle
V^{\mu\nu}\left(h^{\alpha\gamma}u^\beta u_\gamma
-u_\gamma u^\beta h^{\alpha\gamma}\right)\right\rangle,\\
\mathcal O^3_{VPPP}={}&i\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\left\langle
V^{\mu\nu}\left(u_\gamma h^{\alpha\gamma}u^\beta
-u^\beta h^{\alpha\gamma}u_\gamma\right)\right\rangle,\\
\mathcal O^4_{VPPP}={}&\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\left\langle
\{V^{\mu\nu},u^\alpha u^\beta\}\chi_-\right\rangle,\\
\mathcal O^5_{VPPP}={}&\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\left\langle
u^\alpha V^{\mu\nu}u^\beta\chi_-\right\rangle,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $h_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu u_\nu+\nabla_\nu u_\mu$. The $g_i$
normalization and operator definitions above are literal source conventions;
conversion to the repository's canonical Levi-Civita and odd-coupling
convention is governed by the [convention record](../../rcht/odd-parity/levi-civita-convention.md).

# On-shell ideal-omega result

Appendix B.1 gives the local term in the ideal-mixing $
\omega\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ amplitude. In the source's displayed tensor
convention, its scalar coefficient multiplying
$i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}k_1^\alpha k_2^\beta
k_3^\rho\varepsilon_\omega^\sigma$ is
$$
\mathcal F^{\rm local}_{\omega,\rm source}=
\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{M_\omega F^3M_V}
\left[(g_1-g_2-g_3)(M_\omega^2-3m_\pi^2)
+3m_\pi^2(2g_4+g_5)\right].
$$
Thus this on-shell channel depends on the two combinations
$g_1-g_2-g_3$ and $2g_4+g_5$ before additional model constraints. The
paper obtains $2g_4+g_5=-0.60\pm0.02$ after using its earlier short-distance
relations, its assumed $G_V=F_V/F^2$, and the measured branching fraction
(Appendix B.1). This number is conditional on that model and convention; it
is not an independent determination of the local coefficient for the ALP
calculation.

The paper does not specify a general off-shell $V_i\to3\pi$ tensor source.
Its on-shell formula therefore does not fix regular terms or the local
projector contribution of an off-shell antisymmetric-tensor propagator.

Displayed tensors and odd couplings use $\epsilon^{0123}=+1$ and
$\epsilon_{0123}=-1$ for $(+---)$. The canonical coefficients are
$c_i=-c_i^R$, $d_i=-d_i^R$ relative to the opposite source convention.
The [conversion audit](../../rcht/odd-parity/levi-civita-convention.md)
derives the action-preserving map; archived source text remains literal.


# Exact published $\omega\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ formula

With $k_1,k_2,k_3$ assigned to $\pi^+,\pi^-,\pi^0$ and
$s_{ij}=(k_i+k_j)^2$, the paper gives only the $\rho$-mediated contribution
(including cyclic permutations) as (Eq. (30), source lines 893--925):
$$
i\mathcal M_{\omega\to3\pi}=i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
k_1^\alpha k_2^\beta k_3^\rho\epsilon_\omega^\sigma
\frac{8G_V}{M_\omega F^3}\left[
\frac{m_\pi^2(d_1+8d_2-d_3)+(M_\omega^2+s_{12})d_3}{M_V^2-s_{12}}
+\{s_{12}\to s_{13}\}+\{s_{12}\to s_{23}\}\right].
$$
The corresponding VMD-only phase-space width is (Eq. (31), source lines
927--948):
$$
\Gamma=\frac{G_V^2}{4\pi^3M_\omega^5F^6}
\int ds_{13}\,ds_{23}\,\mathcal P(s_{13},s_{23})[\cdots]^2,
$$
where the limits are $4m_\pi^2\le s_{13}\le(M_\omega-m_\pi)^2$ and
$s_{23}^{\min},s_{23}^{\max}$, and
$$
\mathcal P=\frac1{12}\{-m_\pi^2(m_\pi^2-M_\omega^2)^2-s_{13}s_{23}^2
+(3m_\pi^2+M_\omega^2-s_{13})s_{13}s_{23}\}.
$$
Here $[\cdots]$ is exactly the square bracket in the amplitude above. The
authors stress that a direct $\omega3\pi$ term is also leading in $1/N_C$ and
is required phenomenologically (source lines 949--985); it is not supplied by
the seven/four one-pseudoscalar basis formula quoted here.

This expression generalizes only as a sequential charged-vector-exchange
ansatz after changing the flavor trace, masses, propagators, and external
state normalization. The claim that the direct term is necessary is specific
to this application; a complete channel-by-channel $V\to3P$ basis requires
additional operators because the paper's basis is explicitly complete only
for one pseudoscalar (source lines 431--433).

# Published $\pi\to\gamma\gamma$ result

Ruiz--Femenia, Pich and Portoles use the Wess--Zumino normalization
$$
\mathcal L^{(4)}_{\rm WZ}=-\frac{\sqrt2N_C}{8\pi^2F}
\epsilon_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}\langle\Phi\,\partial^\mu v^\nu\,
\partial^\alpha v^\beta\rangle
$$
(Eq. (21), source lines 657--669), with $\Phi=\lambda^a\phi^a/\sqrt2$.
The source does not give a separate component definition of a transition form
factor, nor an explicit off-shell $q^2$ formula. It states only that the
$t_2$ operator contributes if one photon is off shell (source footnote,
lines 1064--1069). Therefore no published $F_{P\gamma^*\gamma}(q^2)$ should
be inferred from this paper without an additional source or a derivation.

For two on-shell photons, the vector-resonance corrections are (Eq. (34),
source lines 1024--1045):
$$
i\mathcal M^{(a)}=-i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
\epsilon_1^\alpha\epsilon_2^\beta k_1^\rho k_2^\sigma
\frac{8\sqrt2}{3}\frac{e^2}{M_V}\frac{F_V}{F}
\frac{m_\pi^2}{M_V^2}(c_1+c_2+8c_3-c_5)=0,
$$
$$
i\mathcal M^{(b)}=i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}
\epsilon_1^\alpha\epsilon_2^\beta k_1^\rho k_2^\sigma
\frac{8e^2}{3F}\frac{F_V^2}{M_V^2}\frac{m_\pi^2}{M_V^2}
(d_1+8d_2-d_3)
=i\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\rho\sigma}\epsilon_1^\alpha\epsilon_2^\beta k_1^\rho k_2^\sigma
\frac{e^2F}{3M_V^2}\frac{m_\pi^2}{M_V^2}.
$$
The resulting width and correction are (source lines 1050--1059):
$$
\Gamma(\pi\to\gamma\gamma)=\frac{\alpha^2m_\pi^3}{64\pi^3F^2}(1-\Delta)^2,
\qquad
\Delta=\frac{4\pi^2}{3}\frac{F^2}{M_V^2}\frac{m_\pi^2}{M_V^2}.
$$
The first displayed amplitude is zero only after the short-distance constraints;
the second is the surviving two-resonance correction. The exact published
result is thus an on-shell width, not a channel-independent transition-form-
factor formula. Flavor generalization requires expanding the WZW trace and
specifying $\eta$--$\eta'$ and vector mixing; those assumptions are absent here.

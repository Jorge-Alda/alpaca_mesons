The paper calculates strong and electromagnetic isospin violation in all physical $\pi K$ channels in chiral perturbation theory, including one-loop effects. It treats the light-quark mass difference and electromagnetic effects in a common power counting. [Kubis:2001ij]

The analysis emphasizes that isolating only selected mechanisms such as $\pi^0$--$\eta$ mixing can be misleading because different isospin-breaking contributions can cancel in the complete amplitude. [Kubis:2001ij]

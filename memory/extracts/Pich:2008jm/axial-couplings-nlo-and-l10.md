# Axial couplings and $L_{10}$ (Pich:2008jm)

- Lagrangian: $\mathcal{L}_A=\frac{F_A}{2\sqrt2}\langle A_{\mu\nu}f_-^{\mu\nu}\rangle$ with antisymmetric-tensor kinetic term $-\tfrac12\langle\nabla^\lambda R_{\lambda\mu}\nabla_\nu R^{\nu\mu}-\tfrac12M_R^2R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\rangle$, $R=V,A$ (Pich:2008jm). Includes bilinear $\mathcal{L}_{SA}$, $\mathcal{L}_{PA}$, $\mathcal{L}_{VA}$ couplings $\lambda_i^{VA}$ in the form-factor analysis.
- Large-$N_C$ scaling: $F,F_V,G_V,F_A,c_d,c_m,d_m=\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N_C})$ (Pich:2008jm).
- LO Weinberg sum rules: $F_V^2-F_A^2=F^2$, $F_V^2M_V^2-F_A^2M_A^2=0$, giving $F_V^2=F^2\frac{M_A^2}{M_A^2-M_V^2}$, $F_A^2=F^2\frac{M_V^2}{M_A^2-M_V^2}$, $M_A>M_V$.
- NLO in $1/N_C$ (renormalized couplings, $\delta^{(1,2)}_{\rm NLO}$ loop corrections): $F_A^{r\,2}=F^2\frac{M_V^{r\,2}}{M_A^{r\,2}-M_V^{r\,2}}(1+\delta^{(1)}_{\rm NLO}-\delta^{(2)}_{\rm NLO})$.
- Single-multiplet LO: $L_{10}=-\frac{F_V^2}{4M_V^2}+\frac{F_A^2}{4M_A^2}$, $C_{87}=\frac{F^2F_V^2}{8M_V^4}-\frac{F^2F_A^2}{8M_A^4}$; NLO result at $\mu_0=0.77$ GeV: $L_{10}^r=(-4.4\pm0.9)\cdot10^{-3}$, $C_{87}^r=(3.1\pm1.1)\cdot10^{-5}$ (Pich:2008jm).
- Including a second multiplet $V',A'$ is needed to make the $A\pi$ vector form-factor and $V\pi$ axial form-factor constraints on the $\lambda_i^{VA}$ mutually consistent; the $A\pi$/$V\pi$ constraints reproduce the $\langle VAP\rangle$ relations of Cirigliano:2004ue (Pich:2008jm).
- Not read in detail: Appendix A form-factor tables. Unverified.

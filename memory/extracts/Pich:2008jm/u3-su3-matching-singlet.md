# $U(3)$ versus $SU(3)$ treatment

Source: `memory/sources/Pich:2008jm/src/L10.tex`. [@Pich:2008jm]

- The Lagrangian is built from $U(3)$ multiplets with single flavour traces and $N_C$ counting (lines 204, 329-336); couplings $F,F_V,G_V,F_A,c_d,c_m,d_m\sim\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N_C})$, $\lambda^{R_1R_2}\sim\mathcal{O}(N_C^0)$, $\lambda^{R_1R_2R_3}\sim\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{N_C})$.
- Statement (lines 1135-1138): the spin-1 two-point functions get no $U(3)$-singlet Goldstone contribution; "the $\eta_1$ does neither enter at tree-level nor in the one-loop correlators". Hence $L_{10}^r(\mu)^{U(3)}=L_{10}^r(\mu)^{SU(3)}$ and $C_{87}^r(\mu)^{U(3)}=C_{87}^r(\mu)^{SU(3)}$ at LO and NLO in $1/N_C$, by contrast with the $S-P$ correlator (`L8-nlo`, i.e. Rosell:2006dt).
- The flavour sum: two-flavour channel results are multiplied by $n_f/2$ (lines 687-689, 2432-2436). The paper does not state which meson content ($\pi$ only or also $K$, $\eta$) the $n_f/2$ factor implies beyond this counting.
- There is no treatment of the $\eta'$ mass or $U(1)_A$ anomaly in this paper (no hit for these terms); $\eta_1$ is simply absent from the correlator.

# Derivative and non-derivative axion couplings

## Access and verification status

- Georgi:1986df (Phys. Lett. B169 (1986) 73) has no arXiv version; no full text was reachable (searches returned no copy). No archive in `memory/sources/Georgi:1986df/`.
- Only the INSPIRE-HEP abstract was read.

## Verified content (INSPIRE abstract)

The paper discusses the low-energy properties of the invisible axion in an effective field theory language, for the most general model in which the strong CP problem is solved by a Peccei-Quinn symmetry spontaneously broken at a large scale. It distinguishes between

1. model-dependent derivative couplings of the axion, associated with physics at the large scale (these may include flavour-changing effects), and
2. model-independent non-derivative couplings that derive from the QCD anomaly.

The abstract also states that the effects producing a nonzero value of a quantity whose symbol is garbled in the INSPIRE text (unresolved), and a long-range force between baryons, are included in the model-independent non-derivative couplings.

## Unverified

The specific statement that an axial (chiral) rotation of the quark fields moves the derivative axion couplings into the quark mass term (and the anomaly term) is the commonly attributed content of Georgi:1986df, but it is not in the abstract and could not be checked in the paper. It must not be cited as source-verified.

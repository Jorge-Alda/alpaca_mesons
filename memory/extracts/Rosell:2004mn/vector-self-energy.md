# Vector self-energy

The one-loop RChT treatment of the pion vector form factor includes the two-pseudoscalar loop in the vector propagator and in the accompanying vertices and counterterms. Its imaginary part supplies the physical two-pion cut; the loop treatment is not equivalent to inserting a constant width into an otherwise tree-level graph. The calculation is organized as a renormalized next-to-leading-$1/N_c$ amplitude. [@Rosell:2004mn]

The paper provides the RChT precedent for using a Dyson-resummed vector denominator only together with the loop-induced pieces required by the chosen renormalization and short-distance constraints. Applying that construction to $K^*$, omega, or phi requires the corresponding channels and counterterms; the pion-loop result is not a universal four-state fit model. [@Rosell:2004mn]

# U(3) realization of non-leptonic weak operators

Gerard:2005yk, Eqs. (6)–(9), gives the leading $U(3)$ pseudoscalar realization of the $\Delta S=1$ weak Hamiltonian as

$$
\mathcal H_W^{\Delta S=1}=G_8Q_8+G_8^sQ_8^s+G_8^mQ_8^m+G_{27}Q_{27},
$$

where

$$
Q_8=4(L_\mu L^\mu)_{23},\qquad
Q_8^s=4(L_\mu)_{23}\langle L^\mu\rangle,\qquad
Q_8^m=F^4(\chi U^\dagger+U\chi^\dagger)_{23},
$$

$$
Q_{27}=4\left[(L_\mu)_{23}(L^\mu)_{11}
+\frac23(L_\mu)_{13}(L^\mu)_{21}
-\frac13(L_\mu)_{23}\langle L^\mu\rangle\right].
$$

Here $L_\mu$ is the matrix of left-handed current realizations defined in Eq. (7) of Gerard:2005yk; the paper's current-to-meson index convention must be converted when using Alda:2026nrd conventions. The low-energy coefficients $G_8,G_8^s,G_8^m,G_{27}$ are independent matching parameters at this level; chiral symmetry fixes the operator forms, not their values.

The paper identifies $Q_8,Q_8^m,Q_{27}$ as the $U(3)$ extensions of the usual $SU(3)$ octet and 27-plet operators. The term $Q_8^s\sim (L_\mu)_{23}\partial^\mu\eta_0$ is specific to the $U(3)$ theory. In the stated basis, inserting an additional factor $\langle\ln U-\ln U^\dagger\rangle\sim\eta_0$ in $Q_8,Q_8^m$, or $Q_{27}$ violates CPS invariance. Other singlet structures with the pseudoscalar mass combination can be reduced to $Q_8^s$ by the leading equations of motion [Gerard:2005yk, discussion following Eq. (8)].

The weak-mass operator $Q_8^m$ does not contribute to hadronic observables at leading order; its higher-order effects can be absorbed into weak counterterms [Gerard:2005yk, text following Eq. (9)].

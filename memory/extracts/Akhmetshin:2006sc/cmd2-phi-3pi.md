CMD-2 is a targeted $\phi\to\pi^+\pi^-\pi^0$ measurement and should be retained as an independent experiment-level cross-check of the KLOE Dalitz/line-shape input [@Akhmetshin:2006sc]. Its observable constrains the charged/neutral $\rho\pi$ decomposition and the $\phi$ three-pion normalization. No machine-readable covariance matrix is cached; do not infer correlations from the plotted distributions.

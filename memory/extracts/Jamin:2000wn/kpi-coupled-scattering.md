The paper constructs unitarized $I=1/2$ $S$-wave amplitudes for the coupled $K\pi$, $K\eta$, and $K\eta'$ channels through approximately $2\,\mathrm{GeV}$ using chiral amplitudes with resonances and fits to scattering information. [Jamin:2000wn]

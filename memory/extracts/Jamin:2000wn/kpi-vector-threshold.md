# $K^*$ channel

The resonance chiral analysis of $K\pi$ scattering supplies the relevant resonance-based partial-wave setting for the strange vector channel. The $K^*$ contribution is a $P$-wave object, so its absorptive threshold behavior is cubic in the breakup momentum, in contrast to an $S$-wave threshold. [@Jamin:2000wn]

For a vector coupling to two pseudoscalars, the corresponding model-independent threshold form is

$$
\Gamma_{X\to ab}(s)=\Gamma_{X\to ab}(M_X^2)
\frac{M_X^2}{s}
\left[\frac{q_{ab}(s)}{q_{ab}(M_X^2)}\right]^3
\theta\!\left(s-(m_a+m_b)^2\right),
$$

$$
q_{ab}(s)=\frac{\sqrt{\lambda(s,m_a^2,m_b^2)}}{2\sqrt{s}}.
$$

The formula is a kinematic $P$-wave prescription; using it as a complete $K^*$ propagator still requires a real-part subtraction and a declared treatment of coupled channels. [@Jamin:2000wn]

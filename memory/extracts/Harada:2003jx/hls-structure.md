# Hidden Local Symmetry: Structure and Assumptions

Hidden local symmetry rewrites the nonlinear chiral field in terms of two variables with a local redundancy. Gauging that redundancy introduces the vector nonet as gauge bosons of the hidden group. This is an economical realization of chiral symmetry, not a claim that QCD possesses a new microscopic gauge interaction. [@Bando:1987br; @Harada:2003jx]

At leading order the phenomenology is controlled by $F_\pi$, the hidden gauge coupling $g$, and

$$
a=\frac{F_\sigma^2}{F_\pi^2}.
$$

The commonly used value $a=2$ gives the tree-level KSRF relations, universality of the $\rho$ coupling, and vector dominance of the pion electromagnetic form factor. The value $a=2$ is a phenomenological parameter choice rather than a consequence of chiral symmetry. [@Bando:1987br; @Harada:2003jx]

The loop formulation counts $g=O(p)$ and therefore $m_V=O(p)$, allowing HLS to be treated as an EFT order by order. This counting and a chosen truncation of higher-derivative operators are assumptions that must be stated whenever loops or precision claims are made. [@Harada:2003jx]

The anomalous HLS action is not fixed by the anomaly alone: besides the gauged Wess--Zumino--Witten functional it contains gauge-invariant homogeneous terms with independent coefficients. These terms control $VVP$, $VPPP$, and related amplitudes, so strict vector dominance is only a special parameter choice. [@Fujiwara:1984mp]

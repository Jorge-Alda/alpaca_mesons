# Large-$N_c$ and $	heta$

Kaiser and Leutwyler organize the effective action as
$$S_{\rm eff}=N_c^2S_0\{\theta/N_c\}+N_cS_1\{v,a,s,p,\theta/N_c\}+S_2\{v,a,s,p,\theta/N_c\}+\cdots,$$
where $S_1$ contains one flavour trace and $S_2$ at most two (their Eq. (2.7), source lines 406--418). Thus a double-trace term is not forbidden; it is one quark-loop order down in the standard OZI expansion [@Kaiser:2000gs].

The simultaneous delta counting is
$$p=O(\sqrt{\delta}),\qquad 1/N_c=O(\delta)$$
(their Eq. (3.1)). At leading order the singlet source and topological field are governed by
$$\mathcal L_\psi=\frac{F_0^2}{4N_f}D_\mu\psi D^\mu\psi-\frac{\tau_{\rm GD}}2(\psi+\theta)^2,\qquad D_\mu\psi=\partial_\mu\psi-2\langle a_\mu\rangle,$$
their Eq. (3.9), source lines 630--690. The invariant singlet combination is therefore $\psi+\theta$ (equivalently $\langle\log U\rangle+i\theta$ in the convention of the U(3) construction), not an independently shift-invariant $\eta_0$ source [@Kaiser:2000gs].

The source explicitly states that the gluodynamic topological susceptibility $\tau_{\rm GD}$ enters at leading order in the simultaneous expansion (source lines 641--665). A model that keeps $\eta_0$ but omits the $\theta$/$Q$ spurion is consequently not a complete U(1)$_A$-covariant nonet theory at that accuracy [@Kaiser:2000gs].

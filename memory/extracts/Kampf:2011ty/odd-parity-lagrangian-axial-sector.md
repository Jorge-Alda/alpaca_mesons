# Odd-intrinsic-parity RChT with axial resonances (Kampf:2011ty)

- Constructs the general odd-intrinsic-parity RChT Lagrangian with lowest multiplets $V(1^{--})$, $A(1^{++})$, $S(0^{++})$, $P(0^{-+})$, used to saturate the $\mathcal{O}(p^6)$ anomalous LECs of ChPT (Kampf:2011ty). It lists the independent monomials with one axial-vector resonance field (about 16 entries in the table, labelled "Monomials with one axial-vector resonance field").
- Spin-1 fields are antisymmetric tensors. Stated rationale: equivalent in principle to the Proca formalism (Ecker:1989yg at $\mathcal{O}(p^4)$; Kampf:2006yf, not in the bibliography, at $\mathcal{O}(p^6)$), but the tensor couples to $\mathcal{O}(p^2)$ chiral building blocks without derivatives and so needs no extra contact terms to cure the high-energy behaviour; with Proca fields and no contact terms the $\mathcal{O}(p^4)$ even-sector LECs and the $\mathcal{O}(p^6)$ odd-sector LECs cannot be saturated (Kampf:2011ty).
- Nonet content for the axial sector: $a_1$, $f_1$, $K_1$ (together with Goldstone bosons), as listed in the paper's schematic chain $V:\rho,\omega,K^*\sim A:a_1,f_1,K_1\sim S:\sigma,\kappa,a_0,f_0$ (Kampf:2011ty).
- Earlier odd-sector work with axial resonances is credited to Pallante:1992qe, Prades:1993ys, Knecht:2001xc, RuizFemenia:2003hm (Kampf:2011ty); only Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx appears here.

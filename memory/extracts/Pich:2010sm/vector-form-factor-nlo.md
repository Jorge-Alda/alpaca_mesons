# NLO vector form factor

The NLO-$1/N_c$ RChT calculation of the pion vector form factor treats loop effects, renormalization, and short-distance constraints together. It is a controlled example of a dressed rho amplitude, rather than a prescription for changing $M_{rho}^2-s$ into $M_{rho}^2-s-iM_{rho}Gamma_{rho}$ by hand. [@Pich:2010sm]

Its practical lesson is that the real dispersive loop part, local counterterms, and the imaginary part cannot generally be varied independently if low-energy matching and high-energy behavior are to be retained. [@Pich:2010sm]

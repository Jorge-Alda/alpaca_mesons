# Radial-mode decays to pions

The model of [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Sec. 5] includes a complex PQ scalar written as
$$
\varphi=\frac{1+\sigma}{\sqrt{2}}e^{ia}
$$
[@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eq. (9)]. Here $\sigma$ is the dimensionless radial field; its canonically normalized excitation is $\sigma_{\rm can}=f_\varphi\sigma$. In the Murayama charge assignment $(n_u,n_d,n_s)=(1,0,0)$, the up-quark mass term is proportional to $\sqrt{2}\varphi$, while the down- and strange-quark mass terms do not depend on $\varphi$ [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eqs. (3), (7), Sec. 3].

The paper gives the approximate estimate
$$
\Gamma(\sigma\to\pi\pi)\approx\frac{\kappa_u^2}{8\pi}m_\sigma
$$
and says that estimates from quark and pion final states are both approximate and similar [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Sec. 5]. The paper does not provide charge-resolved widths or amplitudes for $\pi^+\pi^-$ and $\pi^0\pi^0$, nor does it state in this estimate whether the shorthand $\pi\pi$ is an exclusive channel or a sum over channels. Therefore this formula is a model-level order-of-magnitude estimate, not a channel-by-channel prediction.

The paper further notes that a dedicated decay analysis would be needed if the pion-mass problem were resolved [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Sec. 5]. The estimate should consequently not be read as a controlled treatment of scalar-isoscalar QCD rescattering or resonance mixing.

# Finite axion–meson mixing in a dynamical PQ model

## Chiral-covariant setup

The paper treats a dynamical complex Peccei–Quinn (PQ) field $\varphi$ whose phase enters the quark-mass matrix,

$$
M_q(\varphi)=\operatorname{diag}\!\left[m_u(\sqrt{2}\varphi)^{n_u},\ m_d(\sqrt{2}\varphi)^{n_d},\ m_s(\sqrt{2}\varphi)^{n_s}\right].
$$

This is a spurion under $U(3)_L\otimes U(3)_R$, $M_q\to LM_qR^\dagger$, while the Goldstone matrix transforms as $U\to LUR^\dagger$. Thus the leading mass term $\operatorname{tr}(M_q U^\dagger)+\mathrm{h.c.}$ remains chiral covariant with the axion phase kept inside the spurion [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eqs. (6)–(7)]. The anomaly term is kept as a function of the singlet phase and $\theta$ [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eq. (6)].

## Quadratic CP-odd sector

Writing $\varphi=(1+\sigma)e^{ia}/\sqrt{2}$ and expanding about the CP-conserving minimum gives a noncanonical phase kinetic term $f_\varphi^2(\partial a)^2/2$. The leading chiral potential is kept as cosines of the complete quark-mass phases, including both $a$ and the meson fields [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eqs. (9), (14)]. Taking its Hessian yields the full symmetric mass matrix in the basis $(a,\pi^0,\eta,\eta')$, Eq. (16), with off-diagonal entries proportional to $f_\pi/f_\varphi$ and diagonal ALP entry proportional to $f_\pi^2/f_\varphi^2$. No expansion in that ratio is made. The physical CP-odd masses therefore require diagonalizing the full quadratic matrix when the mixing is not small.

The PQ transformation shifts $a$ and the neutral meson coordinates together, leaving every cosine phase invariant [@DiLuzio:2026poh, Eq. (15)]. This makes the covariance and the origin of the mixing explicit before any choice of mass eigenstates.

## Scope for generic ALP sources

This is an illustration of the full-matrix method, not the same model as an ALP treated as an external source in a fixed quark-mass matrix: here a dynamical PQ scalar generates the quark masses and its radial mode is part of the theory. In particular, the paper's relation between $f_\varphi$ and the quadratic entries should not be imported into a generic ALP chiral Lagrangian without matching the underlying spurions and kinetic normalization.

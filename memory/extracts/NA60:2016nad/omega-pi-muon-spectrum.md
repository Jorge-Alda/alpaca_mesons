# NA60 $\omega\to\pi^0\mu^+\mu^-$ spectrum

NA60:2016nad reports a precision measurement of the $\omega\to\pi^0\mu^+\mu^-$ electromagnetic transition form factor, together with $\eta\to\mu^+\mu^-\gamma$. The vector channel supplies differential information over dilepton invariant mass, complementing the electron channel and extending to larger $q^2$ because of the muon threshold. Use the tabulated spectrum with the published uncertainties and bin definitions; the eta channel is a different transition form factor. [NA60:2016nad]

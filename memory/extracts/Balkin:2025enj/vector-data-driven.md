# Covariant ALP Vector-Meson and Data-Driven Continuation

Balkin et al. use an HLS-like vector Lagrangian

$$
\mathcal L_V=f_\pi^2\langle(gV_\mu-e_\mu)^2\rangle,
$$

which yields vector--photon mixing, a universal tree-level vector mass, and the $VPP$ interaction. Their numerical calculation replaces universal vector masses by measured masses. [@Balkin:2025enj, Sec. 2.2, Eqs. (17)--(21)]

The anomalous sector contains $PVV$, $PV\gamma$, $P\gamma\gamma$, and $\gamma PPP$ interactions. The explicit $\gamma PPP$ term violates strict vector-meson dominance and is retained to improve the description of $\omega\to3\pi$. [@Balkin:2025enj, Secs. 2.2 and 3.2--3.3, Eqs. (23), (39)--(43)]

For four-pseudoscalar amplitudes, basis-dependent contact and vector-exchange pieces cancel exactly only when the universal Lagrangian vector mass is used. The authors nevertheless use measured masses and widths while imposing this cancellation, which is a phenomenological prescription rather than an exact consequence of the modified Lagrangian. [@Balkin:2025enj, Sec. 3.4, Eqs. (44)--(49)]

Above the chiral region, each chiral vertex is multiplied by a phenomenological form factor fitted to $e^+e^-\to\rho\pi,\omega\pi,K^*K,\phi\eta$ and related data. The continuation assumes that a form factor depends only on the Lorentz class of the external hadrons and the hardest scale, and is real and positive; flavor dependence, softer invariants, and strong phases are neglected. [@Balkin:2025enj, Sec. 4, Eq. (50) and following discussion]

The use of measured pseudoscalar masses with a leading-order chiral mixing formula leaves a residual basis dependence estimated by the authors at up to $O(20\%)$ for order-one rotation parameters in their setup. [@Balkin:2025enj, Sec. 2.2 after Eq. (16)]

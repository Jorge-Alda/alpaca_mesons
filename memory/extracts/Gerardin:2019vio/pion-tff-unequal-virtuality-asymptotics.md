# Factorization input

For spacelike virtualities $q_i^2=-Q_i^2$, Eq. (65) writes the leading
twist form factor as

$$
\mathcal F(-Q_1^2,-Q_2^2)=\frac{2F_\pi}{3}\int_0^1 dx\,
T_H(x,Q_1^2,Q_2^2;\mu^2)\,\varphi_\pi(x;\mu^2),
$$

with normalized pion distribution amplitude. At leading order,
Eq. (66) gives

$$
T_H^{\rm LO}=\frac{1}{xQ_1^2+(1-x)Q_2^2}.
$$

The source defines $\overline Q^2=Q_1^2+Q_2^2$ and
$\omega=(Q_2^2-Q_1^2)/(Q_1^2+Q_2^2)$. With the asymptotic distribution
amplitude $\varphi_\pi^{\rm as}(x)=6x(1-x)$, Eq. (69) is

$$
\mathcal F(-Q_1^2,-Q_2^2)=\frac{4F_\pi}{3\overline Q^2}
\int_0^1 dx\,\frac{6x(1-x)}{1+\omega(1-2x)}
+O(\alpha_s/\overline Q^2,\overline Q^{-4}).
$$

This is a leading-twist, leading-order statement with the asymptotic DA; the
source explicitly notes that at finite scale the unequal-virtuality result
depends on the DA shape. Its Eq. (72) gives an expansion for small $\omega$
including the second Gegenbauer moment. At $\omega=0$, Eq. (71) gives
$\mathcal F(-Q^2,-Q^2)=2F_\pi/(3Q^2)$ at leading order. The source's scalar
phase is converted to the repository's canonical negative TFF convention
when used in the associated derivation [@Gerardin:2019vio].

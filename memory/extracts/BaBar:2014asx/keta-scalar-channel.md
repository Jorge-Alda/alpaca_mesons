In an isobar analysis of $\eta_c\to K^+K^-\eta$, BaBar reports the first observation of $K_0^*(1430)\to K\eta$ and obtains

$$
\frac{\mathcal B(K_0^*(1430)\to K\eta)}
{\mathcal B(K_0^*(1430)\to K\pi)}
=0.092\pm0.025^{+0.010}_{-0.025}.
$$

The Dalitz-plot model represents $K_0^*(1430)$ with a Breit-Wigner amplitude and includes a constant nonresonant amplitude. The paper reports that interference between the nonresonant $S$ wave and $K_0^*(1430)$ makes the sum of their fitted fractions exceed the fitted total $K\eta$ or $K\pi$ $S$-wave fraction. [BaBar:2014asx, Dalitz-model discussion and Sec. VIII, Eqs. (11)--(14)]

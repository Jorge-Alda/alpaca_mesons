The nonlinear chiral scalar-nonet model writes derivative scalar-pseudoscalar-pseudoscalar interactions containing independent $\kappa K\pi$, $\kappa K\eta$, and $\kappa K\eta'$ couplings. In the paper's notation,

$$
\gamma_{\kappa K\pi}=-2A,
$$

$$
\gamma_{\kappa K\eta}
=C\sin\theta_p-\sqrt{2}(C-A)\cos\theta_p,
$$

and

$$
\gamma_{\kappa K\eta'}
=\sqrt{2}(A-C)\sin\theta_p-C\cos\theta_p.
$$

These are the coefficients in the explicit trilinear $N\phi\phi$ interaction. [Fariborz:1999gr, Appendix, Eqs. for the trilinear interactions and $\gamma$ couplings]

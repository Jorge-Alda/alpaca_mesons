# Field-Redefinition-Consistent Phenomenological Resonance Extension

Ovchynnikov and Zaporozhchenko extend a three-flavor ALP chiral model with vector, scalar, and tensor resonances. Their operational prescription transforms every resonance Lagrangian under the same ALP-dependent chiral rotation as the pseudoscalar matrix and combines direct and ALP--meson-mixing terms so that the arbitrary rotation matrix cancels from amplitudes. [@Ovchynnikov:2025gpx, Eqs. (7)--(9) and the surrounding discussion]

For vectors they use the HLS anomalous interactions of Fujiwara et al.; the resulting implementation is a phenomenological resonance model rather than a systematically truncated Resonance Chiral Theory. [@Ovchynnikov:2025gpx, section “Our approach”]

The paper reports that omitting the direct rotation-induced resonance terms can change predicted ALP widths by orders of magnitude. In the invariant implementation, the exclusive width above about $1\,\mathrm{GeV}$ fails to approach the perturbative gluonic width, which the authors interpret as evidence that relevant interactions or channels remain missing. [@Ovchynnikov:2025gpx, Fig. 2 and “ALP decay modes”]

Their validation includes reproducing $\pi^0,\eta,\eta'\to\gamma\gamma$ widths at roughly $10\%$ in the pseudoscalar-like limit, but this test does not by itself validate the resonance sector. [@Ovchynnikov:2025gpx, discussion after Eq. (11)]

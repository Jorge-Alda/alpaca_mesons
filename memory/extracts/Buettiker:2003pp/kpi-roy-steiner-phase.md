The Roy-Steiner analysis determines low-energy $\pi K$ $S$- and $P$-wave amplitudes, including the $I=1/2$ and $I=3/2$ phase shifts, below the matching point $s_m=0.935\,\mathrm{GeV}^2$, or $\sqrt{s_m}=0.967\,\mathrm{GeV}$. Exact elastic unitarity is imposed in this low-energy solution. [Buettiker:2003pp]

The reported scattering lengths include $m_\pi a_0^{1/2}=0.224\pm0.022$ and $m_\pi a_0^{3/2}=-0.0448\pm0.0077$. The solution uses higher-energy information as boundary input and is not presented as a continuation above its matching region. [Buettiker:2003pp]

# Leading Resonance Chiral Lagrangian

Ecker et al. construct a chiral effective theory containing the pseudo-Goldstone octet and explicit meson resonances. Resonances transform homogeneously under the compensator of the nonlinear chiral realization. Their construction is a derivative expansion at fixed resonance content, not ordinary low-energy ChPT with the resonances integrated out. [@Ecker:1988te, Secs. 1--2]

For $U=u^2$ and a chiral transformation $(g_L,g_R)$, the nonlinear realization is

$$
u\longrightarrow g_R u h^\dagger=h u g_L^\dagger,
$$

where $h=h(g_L,g_R,U)$ is the compensator. A resonance multiplet $R$ transforms as $R\to hRh^\dagger$. [@Ecker:1988te, Sec. 2]

With $\langle\cdots\rangle$ denoting the flavor trace, the leading pseudo-Goldstone Lagrangian is

$$
\mathcal L_2^\chi=\frac{F^2}{4}\left\langle u_\mu u^\mu+\chi_+\right\rangle.
$$

The standard covariant tensors used to couple resonances are $u_\mu$, $\chi_\pm$, and $f_\pm^{\mu\nu}$; each transforms homogeneously under $h$. [@Ecker:1988te, Sec. 2]

For a vector multiplet represented by an antisymmetric tensor $V_{\mu\nu}$, the kinetic and mass term is

$$
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{kin}}^V
=-\frac12\left\langle
\nabla^\lambda V_{\lambda\mu}\nabla_\nu V^{\nu\mu}
-\frac{M_V^2}{2}V_{\mu\nu}V^{\mu\nu}
\right\rangle,
$$

and the leading even-intrinsic-parity terms linear in one vector field are

$$
\mathcal L_2^V
=\frac{F_V}{2\sqrt2}\left\langle V_{\mu\nu}f_+^{\mu\nu}\right\rangle
+\frac{iG_V}{\sqrt2}\left\langle V_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu\right\rangle.
$$

Here $F_V$ controls the coupling to external vector sources and $G_V$ controls the leading $VPP$ interaction. The displayed operator set is the lowest-derivative even-parity vector Lagrangian in this representation; it is not the complete RChT action once odd intrinsic parity, symmetry breaking, loops, or additional resonance multiplets are required. [@Ecker:1988te, Sec. 2]

At tree level, eliminating the resonance fields reproduces contributions to the $O(p^4)$ low-energy constants of Goldstone-only ChPT. The phenomenological statement that low-energy constants are resonance dominated is an empirical approximation (“resonance saturation”), not a consequence of chiral symmetry alone. [@Ecker:1988te, Secs. 3--6]

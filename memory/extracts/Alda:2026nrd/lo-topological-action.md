# Source inputs for the neutral EOM calculation

The archived arXiv-v1 `main_arXiv.tex`, lines 500–510, defines

$$
X_{TC}=2i\left(\operatorname{tr}\log u+i c_G\frac{a}{f_a}\right),
\qquad m_0^2=\frac{2N_f\chi_t}{f_\pi^2},
$$

and the topological contribution to the meson action

$$
\mathcal L_{TC}=-\frac{f_\pi^2m_0^2}{4N_f}X_{TC}^2.
$$

The leading meson Lagrangian at lines 549–557 retains
$f_\pi^2\operatorname{tr}(u_\mu u^\mu)/4$,
$f_\pi^2\operatorname{tr}(\chi_+)/4$ and this topological term
[@Alda:2026nrd]. Its quark-spurion conventions are in
[Appendix A](appendix-a-expansion.md); conversion to canonical RChT
fields is in [neutral generators](neutral-meson-generators.md).

The neutral linear EOM and resonance contact implications are new repository
derivations, documented in
[the source audit](../../rcht/odd-parity/odd-trace-source-eom-audit.md).
They are not additional formulae asserted in the source paper.

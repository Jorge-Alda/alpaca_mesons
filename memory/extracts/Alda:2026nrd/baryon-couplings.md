# Baryon couplings in Section V

Section V uses the octet-baryon CCWZ Lagrangian with axial constants $D_B,F_B,S_B$, plus the independent singlet coefficient $\widetilde S_B$ multiplying $\partial_\mu X_{TC}$ [@Alda:2026nrd, Sec. V]. In its first-order no-mixing parametrization, the ALP-only baryon building blocks are

$$
\left.\nabla_\mu\mathcal B\right|_a=-i\frac{\partial_\mu a}{f_a}[c_q^{VS},\mathcal B],\qquad
\left.u_\mu\right|_a=-2\frac{\partial_\mu a}{f_a}\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C),
$$

$$
\left.\partial_\mu X_{TC}\right|_a=-2\frac{\partial_\mu a}{f_a}
\left[c_{GA}+\operatorname{tr}\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C)\right],
$$

where $c_q^{VS}=c_q^V-c_q^S$, $c_{GA}=c_G+\operatorname{tr}c_q^A$, and $\mathcal C=-\tfrac12\{\chi_0,c_q^{AP}\}-\tfrac{m_0^2}{N_f}c_{GA}\mathbf1$. The resulting direct baryon couplings are

$$
c^V_{B_1B_2}=-\operatorname{tr}\!\left(c_q^{VS}[\lambda_{B_1}^\dagger,\lambda_{B_2}]\right),
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
c^A_{B_1B_2}={}&-(D_B+F_B)\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\lambda_{B_1}^\dagger\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C)\lambda_{B_2}\right)\\
&-(D_B-F_B)\operatorname{tr}\!\left(\lambda_{B_1}^\dagger\lambda_{B_2}\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C)\right)\\
&-\left[(S_B+\widetilde S_B)\operatorname{tr}\mathbb M_a^{-1}(\mathcal C)+\widetilde S_Bc_{GA}\right]
\operatorname{tr}(\lambda_{B_1}^\dagger\lambda_{B_2}).
\end{aligned}
$$

These coefficients multiply $\partial_\mu a/f_a$ in Eq. (V.4). The paper states that they depend only on the field-redefinition-invariant combinations $c_q^{VS}$, $\mathcal C$, and $c_{GA}$ and are independent of the quark-field parametrization at the stated order. Its section also distinguishes this low-energy pointlike result from the later high-mass form-factor continuation.

The paper calculates the charged $\pi K$ elastic amplitude at next-to-leading chiral order including both $m_d-m_u$ and electromagnetic isospin breaking. It discusses the subtraction of threshold-singular electromagnetic pieces needed to define the hadronic threshold amplitude. [Nehme:2001wa]

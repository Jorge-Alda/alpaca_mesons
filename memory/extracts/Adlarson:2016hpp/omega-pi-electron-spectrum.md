# A2 $\omega\to\pi^0e^+e^-$ spectrum

Adlarson:2016hpp reports the A2 measurement of the $\omega\to\pi^0e^+e^-$ Dalitz decay and the $\eta\to e^+e^-\gamma$ Dalitz decay. The vector channel provides a differential electron-pair-mass spectrum and is a direct input for a finite-virtuality $V P\gamma^*$ form-factor fit. The eta channel is a separate pseudoscalar transition-form-factor measurement; it is not a $V\to P\ell^+\ell^-$ datum. Use the tabulated spectrum and its stated uncertainties/correlations from the paper rather than reducing the fit input to a published slope alone. [Adlarson:2016hpp]

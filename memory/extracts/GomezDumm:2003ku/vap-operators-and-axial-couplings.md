# $VAP$ operators and $a_1$ couplings (GomezDumm:2003ku)

- Vector and axial-vector octet resonances are described with antisymmetric tensors $R=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\sum_{a=1}^8\lambda_aR_a$, $R=V,A$, with kinetic term $-\tfrac12\langle\nabla^\lambda R_{\lambda\mu}\nabla_\nu R^{\nu\mu}-\tfrac{M_R^2}{2}R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}\rangle$ (GomezDumm:2003ku).
- Chiral-limit couplings $F_V,G_V,F_A$ are those of Ecker:1988te and Ecker:1989yg. The general $\mathcal{O}(p^2)$ $VAP$ interaction is $\mathcal{L}_2^{VAP}=\sum_{i=1}^5\lambda_i\mathcal{O}^i_{VAP}$ with real dimensionless $\lambda_i$ (GomezDumm:2003ku, Eqs. lag21, lag2); the operators coincide with $\mathcal{O}^i_{VA}$ listed in the Cirigliano:2004ue extract.
- The three-pion axial form factors depend on (GomezDumm:2003ku, Eq. lambda0)
$$\lambda_0=-\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\big[4\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\tfrac{\lambda_4}2+\lambda_5\big],\ \lambda'=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\big(\lambda_2-\lambda_3+\tfrac{\lambda_4}2+\lambda_5\big),\ \lambda''=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\big(\lambda_2-\tfrac{\lambda_4}2-\lambda_5\big).$$
- Constraints used: $F_VG_V=F^2$, first Weinberg sum rule $F_V^2-F_A^2=F^2$, and (imposed, then relaxed) $2F_VG_V-F_V^2=0$ from the axial form factor (Ecker:1989yg). With these, vanishing of the $J=1$ axial spectral function at large $Q^2$ requires $2\lambda'-1=0$, $\lambda''=0$ (GomezDumm:2003ku, Eq. rellam). This is the version before the $\langle VAP\rangle$ analysis of Cirigliano:2004ue, whose $\lambda',\lambda''$ differ.
- Source does not address the singlet $f_1$ or the $K_1$ mixing explicitly (octet only); mark as not covered.

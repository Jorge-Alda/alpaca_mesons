# $U(3)$ building blocks

Herrera-Siklody et al. use $U=\exp(i\Phi/f)$ with
$$\det U=\exp(i\sqrt{2N_f}\,\eta^0/f)$$
(their Eqs. (3.1)--(3.2)). Their Eq. (3.5bis) introduces the generic topological invariant $\langle\log U\rangle+\hat\theta$, denoted $X_{\rm HS}$ in this repository. It is not renamed $X_{TC}$: that symbol is reserved for Alda's topological source. The recorded conventions do not establish the normalization and sign map between $\hat\theta$ and Alda's ALP source, so no exact factor relation between $X_{\rm HS}$ and $X_{TC}$ is inferred. Any invariant operator may be multiplied by a function of $X_{\rm HS}$; this is the systematic Herrera-Siklody prescription for singlet/topological-source dependence, not a finite list of new ordinary traces (source lines 672--692) [@Herrera-Siklody:1996tqr; @Alda:2026bar].

Their general lowest-order even-parity example is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L_{(0+2)}={}&-W_0(X_{\rm HS})+W_1(X_{\rm HS})\langle D_\mu U^\dagger D^\mu U\rangle+W_2(X_{\rm HS})\langle U^\dagger\chi+\chi^\dagger U\rangle\\
&+iW_3(X_{\rm HS})\langle U^\dagger\chi-\chi^\dagger U\rangle+W_4(X_{\rm HS})\langle U^\dagger D_\mu U\rangle^2+W_5(X_{\rm HS})\langle U^\dagger D_\mu U\rangle D^\mu\hat\theta+W_6(X_{\rm HS})(D\hat\theta)^2,
\end{aligned}$$
their Eq. (3.7). The $W_{4,5,6}$ terms are explicit multi-trace/topological-source examples [@Herrera-Siklody:1996tqr].

The authors count $\chi=O(p^2)$ and identify the anomaly contribution to the singlet mass as $O(1/N_c)$ (source lines 694--710). They also state that the extra U(1)$_A$ terms carry high powers of $1/N_c$, and that the singlet-only/OZI-violating couplings are $1/N_c$ suppressed (source lines 550--558 and 773--830). This supports setting independent double traces to zero as a declared leading-OZI truncation, but not claiming that symmetry makes them vanish [@Herrera-Siklody:1996tqr].

# Dispersive representation

The dispersive representation used for tau to pi pi neutrino separates the elastic pi pi phase from polynomial and subtraction information and incorporates the rho region without identifying the resonance with a constant-width pole. It is a form-factor representation, so its natural input is the measured or scattering phase and normalization rather than an RChT Lagrangian parameter alone. [@GomezDumm:2013sib]

Its systematic limitations are explicit: above the elastic domain, inelastic channels and additional phases must be modeled or supplied; the subtraction polynomial and high-energy phase continuation are fit inputs. [@GomezDumm:2013sib]

The paper solves coupled dispersion relations for the $K\pi$, $K\eta$, and $K\eta'$ scalar form factors using a three-channel scattering matrix. It finds a strongly suppressed $K\eta$ contribution and a dominant $K\pi$--$K\eta'$ coupled system. [Jamin:2001zq]

The coupled-channel solution differs materially from an elastic Omnes description around the $K_0^*(1430)$ region. [Jamin:2001zq]

Graf et al. construct chiral Hilbert series from field/derivative module
characters, a bosonic plethystic exponential, and spacetime/internal Haar
projections. Their Eqs. (6)–(14) include the inverse derivative generating
function to implement the alternating differential-form treatment of IBP
[@Graf:2020yxt]. Section 3.1 explains the possible cohomological correction
$H=H_0+\Delta H$: absence of corrections above $p^4$ in their mesonic theory
is conjectured, not a general theorem for explicit unconstrained resonances.

Table 1 assigns $u_\mu$ chiral weight one and the dressed scalar spurions and
$f_\pm$ weight two. Tables 2–5 implement disconnected parity and charge
conjugation branches. Even powers of a disconnected group element require
the character of its square, rather than its odd-branch character with all
variables simply squared [@Graf:2020yxt].

Their anomalous sector conserves physical parity: an epsilon tensor is
compensated by odd intrinsic field parity (Sec. 4, anomalous-sector projection).
These statements apply to their mesonic setup; the bounded U(3) tensor-vector
quotient in this repository is a separate calculation.

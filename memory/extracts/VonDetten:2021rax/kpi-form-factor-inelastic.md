The paper constructs a scalar $K\pi$ form factor beyond the elastic region using a low-energy Omnes factor and a unitary coupled-channel resonance model. Its low-energy phase input is dispersively constrained, while higher-energy scattering information includes LASS. [VonDetten:2021rax]

The principal coupled channels are $K\pi$ and $K\eta'$. The paper reports that adding $K\eta$ changes the modulus and phase by less than about one percent in its analysis, and its fitted amplitude remains approximately elastic until the $K\eta'$ region near $1.5\,\mathrm{GeV}$. [VonDetten:2021rax]

# Odd-parity resonance options

Roig and Sanz-Cillero reproduce the leading-$1/N_c$ Ruiz--Femenia basis
$$\mathcal L_{R\chi T}^{\rm odd}\supset\sum_{i=1}^7\frac{c_i}{M_V}\mathcal O^i_{VJP}+\sum_{i=1}^4d_i\mathcal O^i_{VVP},$$
and give all eight monomials in their Table 1 (source Eq. (8), lines 345--440). Their convention is antisymmetric tensor vectors [@Roig:2013baa; @Ruiz-Femenia:2003jdx].

For a larger basis that reaches the $O(p^6)$ odd sector, their Table 2 gives the concrete two-vector terms
$$i\langle V_{\mu\nu}V_{\alpha\beta}\rangle\langle\chi_-\rangle,
\qquad i\langle\{V_{\mu\nu},V_{\alpha\beta}\}\chi_-\rangle,$$
and the derivative $VV u$ terms (source lines 488--512). The first is an explicit double trace and the second a single trace with the same flavour/Lorentz content [@Roig:2013baa]. It is therefore a clean optional SU(3)-breaking/OZI-suppressed extension, rather than an ambiguity hidden in the seven/four leading basis operators.

They state that the two bases are related by partial integration, Bianchi and Schouten identities (source lines 517--525), with, for example,
$$\kappa^{VV}_1=-\frac{d_1}{8n_f},\quad \kappa^{VV}_2=\frac{d_1}{8}+d_2,\quad \kappa^{VV}_3=d_3,\quad \kappa^{VV}_4=d_4.$$
This is useful for cross-checking implementation conventions, but it does not generate the missing $U(3)$ singlet/double-trace coefficients [@Roig:2013baa].

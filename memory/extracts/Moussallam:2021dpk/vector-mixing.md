# Vector mixing beyond ideal mixing

Source: https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09772-8, Sections 3.1–3.3.
Downloaded arXiv source: `memory/sources/Moussallam:2021dpk/source.tar`.

The source uses a vector-field representation; its couplings must be translated
before application to the repository's antisymmetric tensors.
Flavor and nonet breaking enter its Eq. (61) mass action. In its ideal-rotated
basis the mass-squared matrix, Eq. (64), is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
m_\rho^2+2\epsilon_V/3 & -\sqrt2\epsilon_V/3\\
-\sqrt2\epsilon_V/3 & 2m_{K^*}^2-m_\rho^2+\epsilon_V/3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The generator matrices in Eq. (62) are

$$
\lambda_\omega=\operatorname{diag}(\cos\varphi_1,\cos\varphi_1,\sqrt2\sin\varphi_1),\qquad
\lambda_\phi=\operatorname{diag}(\sin\varphi_1,\sin\varphi_1,-\sqrt2\cos\varphi_1),
$$

where $\varphi_1=\varphi_I-\varphi_V$, $\varphi_I=\arctan(1/\sqrt2)$.
The source's ideal phi phase is negative strange; change all phi vertices
consistently if adopting a positive strange phase. [@Moussallam:2021dpk]

The leading mass relation holds within 4%, but extracting the angle from the
omega or phi mass gives respectively $-1.2^\circ$ and $-7.3^\circ$ (Section 3.1).
Its decay-based fit reports $-(3.60\pm0.08)^\circ$, Eq. (83). This is conditional
on that action and adopted decay treatment, including its simple pseudoscalar
mixing. The paper assigns 20% modeling uncertainty to the hadronic VVP inputs
and warns that the simple three-pion treatment does not implement the full
unitarity relations (Section 3.3). This angle is not an independent precision
prior for a fit that reuses those decays. [@Moussallam:2021dpk]

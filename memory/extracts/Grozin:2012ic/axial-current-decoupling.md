# Source and definitions

Inspected Sections 3.1–3.3 of `memory/sources/Grozin:2012ic/src/decbc.tex` (arXiv:1205.6001) [@Grozin:2012ic]. The paper distinguishes $n_f=n_l+n_b+n_c$ full-theory flavors and $n_l$ light flavors; its bare and renormalized current decoupling coefficients are defined in Eqs. (3.2)–(3.5) as $j_n^{(n_f)}(\mu)=\zeta_n^{-1}(\mu',\mu)j_n^{(n_l)}(\mu')+O(1/m_{c,b}^2)$ and $\zeta_n=(Z_n^{(n_f)}/Z_n^{(n_l)})\zeta_n^0$.

# Light non-singlet currents

Section 3.1 treats traceless flavor matrix $\tau$. For $n=3$, the axial current decoupling ratio is related to finite renormalization factors by Eq. (3.11): $\zeta_3/\zeta_1=Z_A^{(n_f)}(\alpha_s^{(n_f)}(\mu))/Z_A^{(n_l)}(\alpha_s^{(n_l)}(\mu'))$, with $\zeta_1=1$. The paper states these ratios are independent of $m_c/m_b$. It does not print the finite $Z_A$ coefficient in this proceedings article; it refers to earlier calculations. This is a non-singlet result and cannot be substituted for singlet matching.

# Light singlet and heavy currents

Section 3.2 says the singlet $j_3$ vertex has an additional diagram, with structure $A_s C_F T_F^2 n_l(n_bm_{b0}^{-6\epsilon}+n_cm_{c0}^{-6\epsilon})(\alpha_{s0}^{(n_f)}\Gamma(\epsilon)/\pi)^3$. Thus the extra singlet-specific light-current contribution first appears at three-loop order, $O(\alpha_s^3)$; the text does not give its finite renormalized coefficient. Section 3.3 defines the heavy-current matching $j_{b0}^{(n_f)}=\zeta_A^0j_{q0}^{(n_l)}$ for the antisymmetric three-gamma current corresponding to axial current. It states $\zeta_A^0$ starts at two loops, hence $O(\alpha_s^2)$, and gives its structural expansion in Eq. (3.16), but this extract does not assign a finite numerical coefficient: the paper says it combines the two-mass function with previously known single-scale results to obtain the renormalized coefficient.

# Limits and applicability

These equations prove the perturbative orders and operator channels stated above. They treat simultaneous charm-bottom decoupling and retain exact mass-ratio dependence in the three-loop terms. They do not supply, in this proceedings paper, a finite singlet light-current threshold constant suitable for direct insertion into the ALP symmetric TFF. The heavy-current result is relevant to a heavy axial source matched onto a light axial current, while the light singlet decoupling correction is distinct. Scheme and scale conversions are required before use with the ALP matrix elements.

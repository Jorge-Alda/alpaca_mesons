The model decomposes the three-body channel as

$$
\mathcal A(a\to K\bar K\pi)=\frac{f_\pi}{f_a}
\left[\mathcal A(a\to S_{K\pi}(K\pi)K)
+\mathcal A(a\to a_0(K\bar K)\pi)\right].
$$

The paper defines $S_{K\pi}$ as the $K\pi$ $S$-wave amplitude, states that it has a large $K_0^*(1430)$ contribution and is not well described by a simple sum of Breit-Wigner terms, and adopts the empirical amplitude measured in its Ref. [46]. [Aloni:2018vki, Supplemental Material, $a\to PPP$ subsection]

For the representative charge mode, the scalar term is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal A(a\to S_{K\pi}(K\pi)K)
={}&\left(\frac{8.2}{\mathrm{GeV}}\right)^2
\langle\boldsymbol{a\{K^+,K^-\}}\rangle\mathcal F_{SPP}(m_a)\\
&\times\left[(p_a\cdot p_{K^-})(p_\pi\cdot p_{K^+})
S_{K\pi}(m_{K^+\pi})
+(p_a\cdot p_{K^+})(p_\pi\cdot p_{K^-})
S_{K\pi}(m_{K^-\pi})\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

The paper says that the displayed $K^+K^-\pi^0$ expression is representative and that the amplitude has the same value for all six physical $K\bar K\pi$ final states, although their $U(3)$ generator expressions differ. It obtains the inclusive rate by multiplying the $K^+K^-\pi^0$ rate by six. The other states are $K^+K_S\pi^-$, $K^+K_L\pi^-$, $K^-K_S\pi^+$, $K^-K_L\pi^+$, and $K_SK_L\pi^0$. [Aloni:2018vki, Supplemental Material, $a\to PPP$ subsection]

The paper states that the empirical fit supplies the shape and phase of $S_{K\pi}$, while its magnitude is set by the $\kappa$ resonance parameters from Ref. [47] and by the form factor $\mathcal F$. [Aloni:2018vki, Supplemental Material, $a\to PPP$ subsection]

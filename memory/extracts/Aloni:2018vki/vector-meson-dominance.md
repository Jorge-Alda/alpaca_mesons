# Vector--vector--pseudoscalar interaction in Aloni:2018vki

The supplemental chiral Lagrangian uses $\boldsymbol\Sigma=\exp(2i\boldsymbol P/f_\pi)$ and the matrices

$$
\boldsymbol P=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}
\pi^0/\sqrt2+\eta/\sqrt3+\eta'/\sqrt6&\pi^+&K^+\\
\pi^-&-\pi^0/\sqrt2+\eta/\sqrt3+\eta'/\sqrt6&K^0\\
K^-&\bar K^0&-\eta/\sqrt3+2\eta'/\sqrt6
\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\boldsymbol V=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}
(\rho^0+\omega)/\sqrt2&\rho^+&K^{*+}\\
\rho^-&(-\rho^0+\omega)/\sqrt2&K^{*0}\\
K^{*-}&\bar K^{*0}&\phi
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Its vector-meson-dominance Lagrangian is

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm VMD}=\frac{g_{VVP}}4\langle\boldsymbol P V^{\mu\nu}\widetilde V_{\mu\nu}\rangle
-ig\langle\boldsymbol V^\mu(\boldsymbol P\partial_\mu\boldsymbol P-\partial_\mu\boldsymbol P\,\boldsymbol P)\rangle
-m_V^2\frac eg\langle\boldsymbol V_\mu\boldsymbol Q\rangle A^\mu,
\qquad g_{VVP}=\frac{3g^2}{8\pi^2f_\pi},\quad g\simeq\sqrt{12\pi}.
$$

The first term supplies the $PVV$ vertex; pseudoscalar--ALP mixing supplies the ALP $VV$ amplitude. The last term supplies vector--photon mixing. In the low-mass VMD regime the phenomenological factor in the transition amplitude is normalized to $\mathcal F=1$.

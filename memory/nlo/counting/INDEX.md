# Power counting and renormalization

Declared field content, $\delta$ counting, included and excluded loop classes, subtraction scheme and scales, counterterm basis, separation of resonance exchange from the local constants $L_i^r(\mu)$, and validity range of the combined $U(3)$ NLO theory with explicit resonances. All alp-gii calculations use these records to define the calculated order.

- [Field content](field-content.md) — pseudoscalar nonet with the topological source, ALP sources, and the vector, axial-vector, scalar and pseudoscalar resonance nonets
- [Power counting](power-counting.md) — combined $\delta$ counting of Goldstone operators, resonance couplings and resonance exchange, with unexpanded resonance propagators
- [Loop classes](loop-classes.md) — included Goldstone loops, excluded resonance loops starting at $\delta$ and their threshold region, and the treatment of resonance widths
